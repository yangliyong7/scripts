# Friends S04E09 口语表达笔记

## 1. 情绪与反应

- **Are you ready to party?**: 你准备好嗨了吗？（美式派对口头禅）
  - *Example*: Ross, quick question for ya. Are you ready to party?
- **Dude alert!**: "哥们"警报！（吐槽两个大男生突然变得幼稚）
  - *Example*: Wow! Okay, dude alert! And who is this guy?
- **I am so excited—I may vomit!**: 我兴奋到快吐了！
  - *Example*: Oh man! I am so excited—I may vomit!
- **Oh my God!**: 我的天啊！（本集反复出现的高频反应词）
  - *Example*: Oh my God! You own an Italian restaurant and you think that tastes good?!
- **God, I feel wild today!**: 天，我今天感觉特别野！
  - *Example*: Kidding! God, I feel wild today!
- **How sad are we?**: 我们有多可悲啊？（三个大男人自嘲）
  - *Example*: (pause) How sad are we?

## 2. 决策与行动

- **fill in for someone**: 顶替某人（临时代班）
  - *Example*: I'm gonna fill in for him as food critic for the Chelsea Reporter.
- **hop on board**: 加入/上车（表示同意加入某件事）
  - *Example*: That was me hopping on board.
- **get off work**: 请假不上班
  - *Example*: I'm gonna call and get off work tomorrow!
- **hook up with you later**: 稍后和你会合
  - *Example*: I have an audition, but I'll definitely hook up with you later.
- **stuck in Chicago**: 被困在芝加哥
  - *Example*: He's not gonna make it, he's stuck in Chicago.
- **take a step back from**: 从某事中退一步 / 暂时抽身
  - *Example*: I think it might be time for me to take a step back from catering.
- **retract**: 撤回（声明/评论）
  - *Example*: All right, that I'll retract. But I stand by my review.
- **stand by my review**: 坚持我的评价
  - *Example*: But I stand by my review, I know food and that wasn't it.
- **create a position for you**: 为你专门设一个岗位
  - *Example*: What if I, create a position for you?
- **effective tomorrow**: 明天起生效
  - *Example*: You can have your own office, and a raise! Effective tomorrow.
- **balance my checkbook**: 核对支票账簿（美国人打发时间的老派活动）
  - *Example*: No! I balanced my checkbook.

## 3. 社交与关系

- **the funest guy in the world**: 全世界最好玩的人
  - *Example*: Mike "Gandolf" Ganderson, only like the funest guy in the world.
- **We never party anymore**: 我们再也不出去嗨了
  - *Example*: This is gonna be soo cool, dude, we never party anymore!
- **a big step down for me**: 对我来说是很大的降级
  - *Example*: Okay, but that would actually be a big step down for me.
- **at least one friendly face**: 至少有一张熟悉/友好的脸
  - *Example*: So there'll be at least one friendly face.
- **incredible mentor to me**: 我的良师益友
  - *Example*: Yes, Joanna really has been an incredible mentor to me.
- **cultivating personal relationships**: 培养人脉/私交
  - *Example*: A big part of this job is cultivating personal relationships.
- **getting a little too friendly**: 关系变得太亲密（隐晦地暗示越界）
  - *Example*: The only problem might be getting a little too friendly, if you know what I mean.
- **hear me out**: 把我的话听完
  - *Example*: I'm glad you decided to hear me out.
- **I made a commitment to you**: 我答应过你的（承诺）
  - *Example*: Y'know I-I made a commitment to you.
- **the reason you're unhappy**: 让你不开心的原因
  - *Example*: I don't want to be the reason you're unhappy.

## 4. 描述事物

- **impressive resume**: 令人印象深刻的简历
  - *Example*: You have a very impressive resume, Ms. Green.
- **implementing a new filing system**: 推行一套新的档案分类系统
  - *Example*: I especially like what I see here about implementing a new filing system.
- **You really laid into this place**: 你真是把这家店骂惨了（lay into = 猛烈批评）
  - *Example*: Wow! You really laid into this place.
- **a penny a word**: 一个字一分钱（形容稿费低廉）
  - *Example*: Hey, they don't pay me a penny a word to make friends.
- **inedible swill**: 难以下咽的泔水
  - *Example*: I want a retraction! Our food is not inedible swill!
- **grossly incompetent**: 极其不称职
  - *Example*: Well our service is not grossly incompetent.
- **it feels like it's snowballing**: 感觉像滚雪球一样越滚越大
  - *Example*: It just, it feels like it's snowballing, y'know?
- **run my own kitchen**: 掌管我自己的厨房
  - *Example*: Can you believe it? I finally get to run my own kitchen!
- **an office with walls**: 一间带墙的办公室（相对于隔断工位）
  - *Example*: I'm gonna have an office with walls and everything.
- **I'm kinda beat**: 我有点累坏了
  - *Example*: I'm kinda beat.
- **my ears are ringing**: 我耳鸣了（吵翻天以后的后遗症）
  - *Example*: I can't hear a word you're saying, my ears are ringing so bad.
- **strained my voice**: 嗓子喊哑了
  - *Example*: I think I strained my voice screaming in there.
- **go to bed at a reasonable hour**: 早点睡觉（合理的时间上床）
  - *Example*: I want to sit in a comfortable chair, and watch television and go to bed at a reasonable hour!

## 5. 实用短句

- **quick question for ya**: 快速问你一个问题
  - *Example*: Ross, quick question for ya.
- **I was wondering if I could ask you something**: 我想问你一件事
  - *Example*: Joanna I was wondering if I could ask you something.
- **What if…**: 如果……怎么样？（提议）
  - *Example*: What if I, create a position for you?
- **Say more things like that**: 多说点这样的话吧
  - *Example*: Say more things like that.
- **Done!**: 成交！/ 一言为定！
  - *Example*: I'd need an expense account. — Done!
- **by any chance**: 有没有可能……（委婉打听）
  - *Example*: But by any chance did Joanna send any paperwork your way…
- **I stand by my review**: 我坚持我的看法
  - *Example*: But I stand by my review.
- **let's do the catering business**: 我们还是搞外烩生意吧
  - *Example*: Y'know what, let's do the catering business.
- **make a scene**: 大吵大闹 / 撒泼
  - *Example*: Rachel, please, don't make a scene.
- **crossed the line into completely useless**: 已经完全跨过了没用的界线（讽刺）
  - *Example*: Congratulations! You now just crossed the line into completely useless.
- **may as well just go away**: 你还不如走开
  - *Example*: I'm never gonna let you up so you may as well just go away.
- **without much further ado**: 废话不多说
  - *Example*: So without much further ado, I present to you our new head chef.

## 6. 剧情词汇

- **Gandolf**: 剧中 Chandler 和 Ross 疯狂崇拜的派对之王，人物姓 Ganderson，绰号来自《魔戒》巫师 Gandalf（此处拼写故意错）
- **Kathy**: Chandler 当时的女朋友
- **Chelsea Reporter**: 曼哈顿切尔西区的社区周报，Monica 兼职食评的地方
- **Cilantro Larry**: Monica 烹饪学校的同学，"什么菜都放香菜"的家伙
- **The Discovery Channel**: 探索频道，Ross 想看蜜蜂纪录片
- **Lord of the Rings**: 《魔戒》，Ross 用来向 Joey 解释 Gandolf 的典故
- **Bloomingdale's**: Rachel 工作的百货商场
- **Joanna**: Rachel 的上司（占有欲很强、爱刁难人）
- **Sophie**: Joanna 的另一个助理
- **Junior Miss**: Bloomingdale's 里的青少女服饰部门，Rachel 想去做 assistant buyer
- **assistant buyer**: 助理采购，Rachel 追求的新职位
- **Mr. Posner / Mrs. Lynch**: Bloomingdale's 招聘委员会成员
- **Allesandro's**: Monica 差评的那家意大利餐厅
- **Allesandro**: 餐厅老板，被 Monica 的番茄酱征服，反过来聘她做主厨
- **head chef**: 主厨，Monica 拿到的新工作
- **catering business**: 外烩/私厨上门服务，Monica 和 Phoebe 合伙做的生意
- **Dodge van**: 道奇厢式车，Monica 和 Phoebe 买来跑外烩的老车（车身画着骑龙女郎）
- **Kenny G**: 美国萨克斯风演奏家，Ross 泡澡时爱听
- **A-Team**: 美剧《天龙特攻队》，Phoebe 提到"新 A-Team" 的梗
- **Nova Scotia**: 加拿大新斯科舍省，Chandler 说和 Gandolf 会突然坐渔船去那里
- **snake bite kit**: 蛇咬急救包，Ross 出发前很浮夸地准备的东西
- **decaf**: 无咖啡因咖啡（老派/怕失眠人士的选择）
