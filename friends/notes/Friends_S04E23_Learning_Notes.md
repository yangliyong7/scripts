# Friends S04E23 口语表达笔记

## 1. 情绪与反应

- **That's a lot of ...**: “……也太多了”，表示某人或某事让人有点吃不消。
  - *Example*: Six-hour trip to London. That's a lot of Monica.
- **Oh my God!**: “天哪！”，表达震惊或突然意识到某事。
  - *Example*: Oh my God! Oh my-why didn't you tell me?!!
- **be bummed about ...**: 因……感到沮丧、失落（口语）。
  - *Example*: I'm just bummed about the way I left things with Ross.
- **don't be so hard on yourself**: 别对自己太苛刻。
  - *Example*: Eh, don't be so hard on yourself.
- **I've had a really lousy day**: 我今天过得糟透了。
  - *Example*: And if it makes you feel any better, I've had a really lousy day.
- **I like you less!**: 我更不喜欢你了；常用作带情绪的回怼。
  - *Example*: Well, I like you less!
- **Everything's gonna be all right**: 一切都会没事的；用于安慰。
  - *Example*: It's all right! Everything's gonna be all right.
- **No good can come of this**: 这不会有好结果；表示强烈担忧或反对。
  - *Example*: No good can come of this.

## 2. 决策与行动

- **give that a try**: 试试看。
  - *Example*: Yeah, okay, give that a try!
- **take a couple of days off**: 请几天假。
  - *Example*: Why-why can't you take a couple of days off?
- **put ... off for a bit**: 把……推迟一阵子。
  - *Example*: But, Monica and I were talking, and-and I was so upset about the hall being knocked down, and she suggested that we put the wedding off for a bit.
- **not an option**: 不是可选方案；明确排除某种做法。
  - *Example*: The only thing I understand is; postponing it is not an option.
- **make an informed decision**: 在掌握充分信息后作决定。
  - *Example*: He deserves to have all the information and then he can make an informed decision.
- **step aside**: 让到一边；服务场景中可用于请人离开柜台。
  - *Example*: I'm afraid I'm going to have to ask you to step aside, Miss.
- **do the right thing**: 做正确的事。
  - *Example*: Just uh, do the right thing.
- **get some distance, clear your head**: 暂时离开、理清思绪。
  - *Example*: I think you should go, by yourself, get some distance, clear your head, I think it'd be really good.
- **stay in one place**: 待在一个地方不动。
  - *Example*: No, from now on, I'm staying in one place.

## 3. 社交与关系

- **It'll mean so much to me**: 这对我意义重大；用于真诚邀请或请求。
  - *Example*: Please? It'll mean so much to me.
- **might as well**: 不妨、干脆；既然条件允许就顺便做。
  - *Example*: Oh, and great! You might as well bring me my book, it's on the counter in your apartment.
- **We talk about it all the time**: 我们一直都在谈这件事。
  - *Example*: Yeah, we all know! We talk about it all the time!
- **What's with the third degree?**: 干吗像审犯人一样盘问我？（口语）。
  - *Example*: No!! Okay!! What's with the third degree?! Why don't you just shine a light in my eyes?!
- **get to know you better**: 更深入地了解你；常用于表达交往兴趣。
  - *Example*: I was hoping to get to know you better.
- **no matter what happens**: 无论发生什么。
  - *Example*: Hey, no matter what happens with Ross and Emily, we still get cake right?
- **I hope you don't take this the wrong way**: 希望你别误会我的意思；用于引出敏感话题。
  - *Example*: Oh wow, I hope you don't take this the wrong way but, I know we had plans to meet up tonight and, ugh, I'm just kinda worried about what it might do to our friendship.
- **what it might do to our friendship**: 这可能会怎样影响我们的友谊。
  - *Example*: I'm just kinda worried about what it might do to our friendship.
- **I could use a friend**: 我现在需要一个朋友陪伴或帮忙。
  - *Example*: No really, I mean, I mean, God, I could use a friend.
- **meant a lot to me**: 对我意义很大。
  - *Example*: Well, an-anyway, I just-that night meant a lot to me, I guess I'm just trying to say thanks.

## 4. 描述事物

- **Hands down, best ...**: 毫无疑问是最好的……（口语强调）。
  - *Example*: All right! Westminster Abbey! Hands down, best Abbey I've ever seen.
- **the most lovely ... you'll ever see**: 你所见过最可爱的……；最高级描述句型。
  - *Example*: It really is the most lovely building you'll ever see.
- **a spectacular amount of ...**: 多得惊人的……。
  - *Example*: What should be the most wonderful day of my life is rapidly turning into just Sunday with a spectacular amount of food!
- **more ... than ...**: 与其说……不如说……；用于对比特征。
  - *Example*: I don't know, his uh, his hair never really bothered me that much, and y'know it was always more crunchy than it was greasy.
- **kinda dashing**: 有点英俊潇洒（非正式）。
  - *Example*: Joey says you don't really like his hat, but I think it's kinda dashing.
- **like a fairyland**: 像仙境一样。
  - *Example*: Oh my God! It's like a fairyland.
- **sound proof**: 隔音的；源台词采用分写形式。
  - *Example*: I know, I've been looking at those doors, they look pretty sound proof, don't you think?
- **over international waters**: 在国际水域上空。
  - *Example*: Y'know, technically we still are over international waters.
- **an unsuccessful, regular sized man**: 一个事业失败、身材普通的人；Joey 的夸张自我描述。
  - *Example*: I mean, there's no way I can make myself taller now, y'know? And who knows what science will come up with in the future, but Chandler, what if I die an unsuccessful, regular sized man?

## 5. 实用短句

- **Here we go.**: 开始吧／出发吧。
  - *Example*: It's all London, baby! Here we go.
- **What the hell is that?**: 那到底是什么？（语气较强）。
  - *Example*: Oh, what the hell is that?
- **I got it!**: 我明白了／我搞定了。
  - *Example*: I got it!
- **That is all you.**: 那只能你自己处理；表示不愿插手。
  - *Example*: Okay, that is all you.
- **Good choice.**: 选得好。
  - *Example*: Good choice.
- **Wait up!**: 等等我！（口语）。
  - *Example*: Wait up!
- **for the record**: 郑重说明一下；用于澄清立场。
  - *Example*: Okay. Umm, before we get started, I just wanna say for the record that I love Ross, I think he's such a great guy.
- **I'm not at liberty to divulge that information**: 我无权透露该信息（正式表达）。
  - *Example*: I'm afraid, I'm not at liberty to divulge that information.
- **Out loud?**: 是笑出声了吗？／有说出口吗？
  - *Example*: Out loud?
- **Same difference.**: 反正都一样；表示两者没实质区别。
  - *Example*: Same difference.
- **The man's got a point.**: 他说得有道理。
  - *Example*: The man's got a point.
- **That counts!**: 那也算数！
  - *Example*: That counts!

## 6. 剧情词汇

- **Ross Geller**: 新郎；婚礼誓词中误把 Emily 叫成 Rachel，导致婚姻危机。
- **Emily Waltham**: Ross 的英国新娘；婚礼后因口误离开，并在机场再次看到 Ross 与 Rachel 同行。
- **Rachel Green**: 意识到仍爱 Ross 后飞往伦敦，却在婚礼前选择只说“Congratulations”。
- **Monica Geller & Chandler Bing**: 在伦敦意外开始秘密关系，并约定回纽约后停止。
- **Joey Tribbiani**: 沉迷伦敦观光、结识伴娘 Felicity，也负责阻止 Rachel 干扰婚礼。
- **Phoebe Buffay**: 因怀孕留在纽约，通过电话试图阻止 Rachel；化名 Dr. Philange。
- **London Marriott**: 众人在伦敦入住的酒店，也是 Monica 与 Chandler 感情线的重要地点。
- **Westminster Abbey**: Joey 观光拍摄的伦敦地标，引出“Westminster Crabby”双关。
- **The Waltham House**: Emily 父母的住所与彩排晚宴相关地点。
- **John F. Kennedy International Airport**: Rachel 从纽约临时购买伦敦机票的机场。
- **Flight 1066 to Athens**: Ross 与 Emily 原定蜜月航班；Rachel 登机后 Emily 才赶到。
- **Fergie / Duchess of York**: Joey 偶遇并拍进录像的英国王室名人。
- **Cheers**: Joey 在伦敦电视上看到的美国情景喜剧；主题曲让他更加想家。
- **Titanic**: Chandler 被醉汉误认参演过的电影，笑点影射演员外形。
- **Monty Hall / Let's Make a Deal**: Chandler 敬酒时使用的美国电视节目文化梗，在场宾客无人响应。
- **My Giant**: Joey 喜欢的电影，也是 Monica 与 Chandler 用来掩饰独处的借口。
- **pop-up map of London**: Joey 的立体伦敦地图；他真的站进地图里辨认方向。
- **Joey Special**: 两份披萨；Phoebe 用它表示大家在纽约想念 Joey。
- **Chicken Kiev / Chicken Tarragon**: 婚宴临时更换的鸡肉菜式，体现筹婚混乱。
- **wedding hall / honeymoon suite**: 被提前拆毁的婚礼场地与无人使用的蜜月套房，推动婚礼和秘密恋情两条剧情。
