# Friends S04E14 口语表达笔记

## 1. 情绪与反应

- **What are you doing with your life?**: 你的人生打算怎么办？（长辈/朋友质问式关心）
  - *Example*: Joey, what are you doing with your life?
- **I would never miss Phase Two**: 我绝不会错过第二阶段。
  - *Example*: I’ll be back for Phase Two, I would never miss Phase Two.
- **keep her company**: 陪她/给她作伴。
  - *Example*: I was wondering if you’d like to keep her company this evening.
- **Thank goodness**: 谢天谢地。
  - *Example*: Thank goodness.
- **I think you look great**: 我觉得你看起来很好/状态不错。
  - *Example*: I-I-I think you look great.
- **I overslept**: 我睡过头了。
  - *Example*: Oh my God! I overslept!

## 2. 决策与行动

- **fancy club opening**: 高档俱乐部开业。
  - *Example*: It’s Joshua invited me to this fancy club opening tonight.
- **teeny-teeny change in plans**: 计划有了一点点小变动。
  - *Example*: There’s been a teeny-teeny change in plans.
- **I must’ve missed your call**: 我一定是错过了你的电话。
  - *Example*: I must’ve missed your call, even though I didn’t leave the flat all day.
- **I did punch a girl in the face**: 我确实打了女孩的脸（did 表强调）。
  - *Example*: Well, I didn’t see Joshua last night, but I did punch a girl in the face.
- **I clocked her**: 我揍了她（clock 俚语“打”）。
  - *Example*: This big bitch behind me tried to steal my umbrella, so I clocked her.
- **getting nowhere with**: 和……毫无进展。
  - *Example*: I’m just upset that I’m getting nowhere with Joshua.
- **go flitting off to**: 飞往/匆匆去某地。
  - *Example*: You do not meet someone and go flitting off to Vermont!
- **bust into**: 闯入/破门而入。
  - *Example*: You must never, ever bust into my dressing room and use my shower!

## 3. 社交与关系

- **What’s Phase Two?**: 第二阶段是什么？（Chandler 失恋后的恢复阶段）
  - *Example*: Gettin’ drunk and going to a strip club.
- **picturing yourself with other women**: 想象自己和别的女人在一起。
  - *Example*: Which helps him get to Phase Three, picturing yourself with other women.
- **We can be guys**: 我们可以像男生一样。
  - *Example*: Yeah, come on, we can be guys!
- **stop being such a wuss**: 别这么怂/别这么窝囊。
  - *Example*: You just stop being such a wuss and get those off.
- **Phase Three is a lost cause**: 第三阶段没戏了。
  - *Example*: We tried, but Phase Three is a lost cause.
- **I couldn’t picture myself with any of them**: 我无法想象自己和她们任何一个在一起。
  - *Example*: Those strippers were insanely hot, and I couldn’t picture myself with any of them.
- **I just achieved Phase Three**: 我刚达到第三阶段了。
  - *Example*: Phase Three! I just achieved Phase Three!
- **I don’t want to have a relationship ever**: 我再也不想谈恋爱了。
  - *Example*: I don’t want to have a relationship ever! I just want to have sex with strippers and my friends!!
- **waiting to screw me over**: 等着伤害我。
  - *Example*: There are literally thousands of women out there just waiting to screw me over.
- **We’re in a place where we could finally be happy for each other**: 我们终于可以为彼此感到高兴了。
  - *Example*: What you said, about us being in a place where we could finally be happy for each other.
- **I’m finally where you are**: 我终于和你一样了/我终于理解你了。
  - *Example*: I’m totally there! I’m-I’m finally where you are!

## 4. 描述事物

- **pouring down rain**: 倾盆大雨。
  - *Example*: The whole night was horrible, it was pouring down rain.
- **the elastic on my underwear busted**: 我内裤的松紧带断了。
  - *Example*: Oh no wait, oh no, the elastic on my underwear busted.
- **Run ten blocks, that’ll help the smell**: 跑十个街区，这样味道会更重（讽刺）。
  - *Example*: Yeah. Run ten blocks, that’ll help the smell.
- **the man wreaks / reeks**: 这男人很臭。
  - *Example*: Yeah, the man wreaks! Smells like he went on a three day fishing trip and then ate some liquorice.
- **insanely hot**: 火辣得不可思议。
  - *Example*: Those strippers were insanely hot.
- **no words to describe it**: 无法形容。
  - *Example*: Emily is incredible. I mean there-there are no words to describe it.
- **the whole weekend was like a dream**: 整个周末像梦一样。
  - *Example*: I mean the whole weekend was like a dream.

## 5. 实用短句

- **I already told...**: 我已经告诉过……
  - *Example*: I already told Mr. Waltham that I would take his niece to this dumb old opera.
- **What are you gonna do?**: 你打算怎么办？
  - *Example*: So… What are you gonna do?
- **I would, but...**: 我很想，但是……（委婉拒绝）
  - *Example*: I would, but I get my morning sickness in the evening.
- **This is my one chance for him to see...**: 这是我唯一一次机会让他看到……
  - *Example*: This is my one chance for him to see the fun Rachel.
- **I thought we had moved on**: 我以为我们已经翻篇了。
  - *Example*: I thought we have moved on!
- **for once, not remember every little thing**: 就一次，别记住每件小事。
  - *Example*: Would you just for once, not remember every…little…thing!!
- **You can’t just go back a phase**: 你不能倒退一个阶段。
  - *Example*: Chandler! You can’t just go back a phase!
- **You’re thinking about time**: 你在想时间的问题。
  - *Example*: You’re thinking about time, you can’t go back in time.
- **running a little late**: 有点晚了/迟到了一会儿。
  - *Example*: Calm down, we got time, we’re running a little late.
- **I’m ready to get drunk and see some strippers**: 我准备好喝醉看脱衣舞娘了。
  - *Example*: That’s right! Where are the guys? I’m ready to get drunk and see some strippers.
- **Happy for you**: 为你高兴。
  - *Example*: Happy for you. (He punches her back.)

## 6. 剧情词汇

- **Joey**: 去钓鱼三天没洗澡，还摔进虫子桶，片场被 Charlton Heston 撞见偷用淋浴
- **Chandler**: 和 Kathy 分手后经历 Phase One（运动裤）、Phase Two（脱衣舞俱乐部）、Phase Three（想象和别人在一起）
- **Rachel**: Bloomingdale’s 的同事 Joshua 约她去夜店，但她要陪 Mr. Waltham 的侄女 Emily 看歌剧
- **Ross**: 被迫替 Rachel 陪 Emily 看歌剧，结果两人去了 Vermont 的 Bed and Breakfast
- **Emily**: Mr. Waltham 从伦敦来的侄女，后来成为 Ross 的女友
- **Joshua**: Rachel 暗恋的 Bloomingdale’s 顾客/同事
- **Mr. Waltham**: Rachel 在 Bloomingdale’s 的老板
- **Charlton Heston**: 美国传奇演员，Joey 正在拍他的戏
- **Bloomingdale’s**: Rachel 工作的高端百货商场
- **Die Fledermaus**: 歌剧《蝙蝠》，Mr. Waltham 给的票
- **Vermont**: 美国东北部州，Ross 和 Emily 去度假的地方
- **Bed and Breakfast**: 住宿加早餐的小旅馆
- **Silvercup Studios**: 纽约的影视制片厂
- **liquorice whip**: 甘草鞭糖，Charlton Heston 在拍摄现场吃的零食
- **The X-Files**: 美剧《X档案》，剧中提到“抽烟的男人”梗
- **strip club**: 脱衣舞俱乐部
