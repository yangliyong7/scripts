# Friends S04E02 口语表达笔记

## 1. 情绪与反应

- **I can't believe...**: 我真不敢相信……（表达惊讶、不满或难以接受）
  - *Example*: I can't believe you don't know what I do for a living!
- **That's gotta be embarrassing!**: 那一定很尴尬！（gotta be 是口语化的“肯定是”）
  - *Example*: Oh, that's right! He called to ask out Monica! That-that's gotta be embarrassing!
- **Are you insane?**: 你疯了吗？（对离谱想法表示强烈质疑）
  - *Example*: Ahh, Gepeto, $5,000 dollars? Are you insane?
- **That's so sweet.**: 太温馨了/太贴心了。（可真诚，也可像 Joey 此处一样因不知如何回应而敷衍）
  - *Example*: That's so sweet. (pause) I'm gonna get some coffee.
- **I've never felt better about myself.**: 我从来没有这么自信、这么自我感觉良好过。
  - *Example*: I've got a great job at Bloomingdale's, have wonderful friends, and eventhough I'm not seeing anyone right now, I've never felt better about myself.
- **Enough is enough!**: 够了，不能再这样了！（表示忍耐已到极限）
  - *Example*: No! No! Look—Hey, enough is enough!
- **I hate when Ross is right!**: 我最讨厌 Ross 说对了！（不情愿承认对方正确）
  - *Example*: I hate when Ross is right!
- **Oh honey, I'm sorry.**: 哦，亲爱的，真遗憾。（安慰熟悉的人，语气亲密）
  - *Example*: Oh honey, I'm sorry.

## 2. 决策与行动

- **get rid of something**: 处理掉/摆脱某物。
  - *Example*: So you guys having any luck getting rid of the entertainment center?
- **work out**: 成功、产生理想结果（此处指潜在买家不合适）。
  - *Example*: Well, there were a couple of calls last night, but ah, I don't think any of them are gonna work out.
- **be willing to trade for it**: 愿意拿别的东西来交换它。
  - *Example*: We don't have 50 bucks, but would you be willing to trade for it?
- **throw it away**: 把它扔掉。
  - *Example*: All right, just, just take the entertainment center, and then when you get home, throw the canoe away!
- **make a sale**: 做成一笔买卖/把东西卖出去。
  - *Example*: I was tryin' to make a sale!!
- **give up that job**: 放弃那份工作。
  - *Example*: Oh, like I'd give up that job!
- **dump someone**: 甩掉某人、主动结束恋爱关系（口语）。
  - *Example*: I got to dump Chip Matthews.
- **respect her decision**: 尊重她的决定。
  - *Example*: I mean, I have to respect her decision. Right?
- **take you home**: 带你回家/送你回去。
  - *Example*: Come on, Mom, I'll take you home.

## 3. 社交与关系

- **ask someone out**: 邀请某人约会。
  - *Example*: He called to ask out Monica!
- **go out with someone**: 和某人约会、谈恋爱。
  - *Example*: Is it okay if I go out with Chip Matthews?
- **owe someone something**: 觉得自己应该为某人做某事、欠某人一个机会。
  - *Example*: I-I owe her this. I never let her eat.
- **be in touch with someone**: 与某人保持联系。
  - *Example*: So you still in touch with anyone from high school?
- **lose touch**: 与人逐渐失去联系。
  - *Example*: Ehh, y'know after high school, you just kinda lose touch.
- **mean a lot to me**: 对我意义重大。
  - *Example*: Mom, I hope you know you still mean a lot to me.
- **be a friend / be supportive**: 尽朋友本分、给予支持。
  - *Example*: Just be a friend. Okay? Be supportive.
- **forgive someone**: 原谅某人。
  - *Example*: Thank you. We both forgive you.
- **be welcome to come back anytime**: 随时欢迎回来。
  - *Example*: And you're welcome to come back anytime.

## 4. 描述事物

- **stunning entertainment center**: 漂亮得惊人的组合娱乐柜（广告式夸张描述）。
  - *Example*: Stunning entertainment center.
- **fine Italian craftsmanship**: 精湛的意大利工艺（Chandler 读广告时刻意停顿，暗示名不副实）。
  - *Example*: Fine, (pause) fine Italian craftsmanship.
- **the finest oak East of the Mississippi**: 密西西比河以东最上等的橡木（夸张、复古的广告措辞）。
  - *Example*: Hey, here's a question; where did you guys get the finest oak East of the Mississippi?
- **lined with orange felt**: 内衬橙色毛毡。
  - *Example*: She went into my guitar case which is lined with orange felt.
- **a very careful screening process**: 非常严格的筛选流程（Chandler 讽刺 Joey 挑买家过于认真）。
  - *Example*: Yes, Joey has a very careful screening process.
- **sturdy construction**: 结构坚固（销售商品时常用的描述）。
  - *Example*: Sturdy construction, tons of storage compartments, some big enough to fit a grown man.
- **tons of storage compartments**: 大量储物格（tons of 表示“非常多”）。
  - *Example*: Sturdy construction, tons of storage compartments, some big enough to fit a grown man.
- **not deep enough**: 不够深。
  - *Example*: That's not deep enough.
- **an empty apartment**: 一间被搬空的公寓。
  - *Example*: He then looks up at an empty apartment; everything is gone except for the entertainment center and that ceramic dog.
- **the reincarnated spirit**: 转世的灵魂。
  - *Example*: When clearly you are also the reincarnated spirit, of my friend's mother.

## 5. 实用短句

- **Give me a call.**: 给我打电话。（日常邀请对方联系）
  - *Example*: Give me a call. Later.
- **I was just leaving.**: 我正准备走。（可用于告辞或缓和碰面时的尴尬）
  - *Example*: I was just leaving.
- **Why don't you give him a call?**: 你为什么不给他打个电话呢？（委婉建议句型）
  - *Example*: Well, umm, why don't you give him a call?
- **Are you sure you wanna hear this?**: 你确定想听吗？（wanna 为 want to 的口语形式）
  - *Example*: Are you sure you wanna hear this?
- **Let it go!**: 别再纠结了/算了吧！
  - *Example*: Let it go!!
- **Don't start.**: 别开始了/别又来了。（阻止对方继续熟悉的争论）
  - *Example*: Ross, don't start.
- **Good for you!**: 干得好/真替你高兴！（可真诚，也可带讽刺）
  - *Example*: Good for you!!
- **How's that going?**: 那件事进展得怎么样？
  - *Example*: How's that going?
- **I'll be right there.**: 我马上过去。
  - *Example*: I'll be right there.
- **Is that all?**: 就这些吗？
  - *Example*: Is that all?
- **I don't know what to say.**: 我不知道该说什么。
  - *Example*: I don't know what to say.
- **That's a good call.**: 这个决定不错/这样处理很明智。（口语中 call 表示判断或决定）
  - *Example*: That's a good call. Right.

## 6. 剧情词汇

- **Chip Matthews**: Monica 和 Rachel 的高中同学；Rachel 当年的舞伴，也是 Monica 多年来想约会的校园风云人物
- **Julio**: 小女孩走失的公猫；Phoebe 相信它承载了已故母亲 Lily 的灵魂
- **Lily Buffay**: Phoebe 已故的养母；Phoebe 因愧疚和思念而把 Julio 当作她的转世
- **Montauk**: 纽约长岛东端地区；Phoebe 在上一集见到生母的地方
- **Central Perk**: 六位主角常去的咖啡馆；众人在这里发现 Julio 的寻猫启事
- **Soho**: 纽约曼哈顿街区；Julio 的小主人住在那里
- **Bloomingdale's**: Rachel 工作的纽约百货公司，也是她向 Chip 展示成年后成就时提到的工作地点
- **Dot's Spot**: Monica 与 Chip 约会用餐的餐厅
- **Orange Roughy**: 橙鲷鱼；Phoebe 用养母最喜欢这种鱼来论证猫就是母亲转世
- **Gepeto**: 《木偶奇遇记》中制造匹诺曹的木匠；Chandler 用这个名字嘲笑把娱乐柜当杰作的 Joey
- **Jurassic Park**: 《侏罗纪公园》；Rachel 用“恐龙复活也可能发生”故意挑起凡事要讲科学的 Ross
- **the Chipper**: Monica 高中时给 Chip 的摩托车起的外号，由 Chip 和 chopper 拼成
- **Home Ec**: Home Economics（家政课）的简称；Monica 的超大号乐队制服竟成了家政课项目
- **letterman jacket**: 美国高中校队主力常穿的字母夹克，象征 Chip 和 Rachel 当年的校园人气
- **entertainment center**: Joey 亲手制作的大型娱乐柜；出售它的计划最终导致公寓遭窃
- **canoe**: 独木舟；买家想用它交换娱乐柜，片尾 Joey 和 Chandler 最终只剩下它
- **foosball table**: 桌上足球台；公寓遭窃后连这件标志性家具也被偷走
- **futon**: 日式折叠床/沙发床；Ross 提到自己有多余的一张，反而让一无所有的 Joey 更受刺激
- **multiplex**: 多厅电影院；成年后的 Chip 仍在高中时期的电影院工作
- **wedgie**: 把别人的内裤从背后向上猛拉的恶作剧，显示 Chip 成年后仍停留在高中生心态
- **tetanus shot**: 破伤风针；Chandler 夸张抱怨破旧娱乐柜会划破衣服甚至伤到人
