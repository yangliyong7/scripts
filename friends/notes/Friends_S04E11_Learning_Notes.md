# Friends S04E11 口语表达笔记

## 1. 情绪与反应

- **I am so jealous**: 我好嫉妒啊。
  - *Example*: (看到 Chandler 和 Kathy 亲热) Monica: I am so jealous.
- **What's your point?**: 你想说什么？（不以为然的反问，意思是"那又怎样"）。
  - *Example*: Ross: ...morning sickness, labour, and it's all for somebody else! Phoebe: Yeah, what's your point?
- **I can't believe...**: 我真不敢相信……
  - *Example*: Oh my God! I can't believe my little brother is married!
- **That is really nice...lying!**: 说得真好听……骗人的！（先夸后拆穿）
  - *Example*: Ross: That is really nice...lying! No way is that the reason!
- **You are so precious**: 你真是太可爱了。
  - *Example*: Alice: You are so precious, I could just take you home.
- **I think I'm gonna cry**: 我觉得我要哭了（感动）。
  - *Example*: Frank: Oh my God, I think I'm gonna cry!

## 2. 决策与行动

- **elope**: 私奔、偷偷结婚（不办婚礼、不告诉家人）。
  - *Example*: You guys, why didn't you tell me you were eloping?
- **get a jump on things**: 抢先下手、提前行动。
  - *Example*: We thought we'd get a jump on things, y'know no one's getting any younger.
- **think it through**: 仔细想清楚、通盘考虑。
  - *Example*: We just want you to think it through.
- **go for it**: 放手去做、大胆上。
  - *Example*: Ross: Just go for it Chandler.
- **I can't give him up**: 我舍不得把它送走。
  - *Example*: I can't do this, I can't give him up.
- **shed these coats that separate us**: 脱掉分隔我们的外套（Ross 号召打破阶层）。
  - *Example*: I say we shed these coats that separate us, and we get to know the people underneath.
- **I'm having my lunch at this table**: 我就要坐这张桌子吃午饭（表态打破规矩）。
  - *Example*: I'm having my lunch at this table, here in the middle, with my good friend Joey.

## 3. 社交与关系

- **fill his shoes**: 比得上他、接得住他的水平（字面"穿他的鞋"）。
  - *Example*: Ross: And you're afraid you won't be able to...fill his shoes.
- **be supportive like crazy**: 全力支持、力挺到底。
  - *Example*: No, Pheebs, listen, if you decide to do this, we'll be supportive like crazy.
- **in the beginning where it's all sex and talking**: 热恋期，整天就是聊天和亲密。
  - *Example*: In the beginning where y'know it's all sex and talking and sex and talking...
- **move on to the next level**: 进入下一阶段（这里指亲密关系升级）。
  - *Example*: I want our love to grow until we move on to the next level.
- **We share a wall**: 我们共用一堵墙（隔壁邻居，什么都听得见）。
  - *Example*: We share a wall! So either he's great in bed, or she just likes to agree with him a lot.
- **back me up**: 帮我说话、给我撑腰。
  - *Example*: It's like that everywhere, Joey! Okay, Mon, back me up here.
- **there's still no pressure**: 还是没有任何压力（给对方空间不做逼迫）。
  - *Example*: We just thought we'd stop by and let you know there's still no pressure.

## 4. 描述事物

- **the greatest gift you can possibly give**: 你能给出的最伟大的礼物。
  - *Example*: I'm gonna be giving them the greatest gift you can possibly give.
- **keep them on their toes**: 让她们保持期待/不敢松懈（字面"让她们踮着脚尖"）。
  - *Example*: You want to hit 'em all, and you mix 'em up. You gotta keep them on their toes.
- **It's like Sophie's Choice**: 就像《苏菲的选择》一样（形容极其艰难的抉择）。
  - *Example*: I can't watch this, it's like Sophie's Choice.
- **a million times harder**: 难一百万倍（夸张对比）。
  - *Example*: It would be like a million times harder to give up a child.
- **I eat by myself in the alley because everybody hates me**: 我一个人躲巷子里吃，因为所有人都讨厌我（Monica 自嘲）。
  - *Example*: I eat by myself in the alley because everybody hates me.
- **Donald Trump wants his blue blazer back**: Donald Trump 想要回他的蓝西装（嘲讽 Joey 穿得像地产大亨）。
  - *Example*: I don't know, but Donald Trump wants his blue blazer black...back.

## 5. 实用短句

- **So be it**: 那就这样吧、随它去。
  - *Example*: ...if that means we can't be friends at work, then so be it.
- **Whatever**: 随便、无所谓（敷衍或赌气）。
  - *Example*: Joey: Whatever.
- **You do what you gotta do**: 你做你该做的（表示理解对方的选择）。
  - *Example*: It's no big deal. Hey, y'know, you do what you gotta do. Right?
- **I don't know squat about...**: 我对……一窍不通（squat＝屁、啥也不是）。
  - *Example*: I'm Joey! I'm an actor! I don't know squat about dinosaurs!
- **hang in there**: 撑住、加油挺住。
  - *Example*: Yeah, you hang in there Teddy!
- **good for you**: 真不错、为你高兴。
  - *Example*: Ross: Okay, good-good for you.
- **Maybe it's crazy in a perfect world**: 也许在完美世界这很疯狂（但现实不是）。
  - *Example*: Maybe it's crazy in a perfect world, a world without lab coats and blazers, but you not in a perfect world, you in a museum now.
- **moving right along**: 好，继续往下走（导游/主持转场的口头禅）。
  - *Example*: Okay, moving right along. Come on.
- **I shared my pudding with you, man**: 我跟你分享过布丁的啊（套近乎翻旧账）。
  - *Example*: I shared my puddin' which you man! I gave you my Snack Pack!

## 6. 剧情词汇

- **Phoebe Sr.**: Phoebe 的生母（birth mom），做陶艺，曾放弃两个孩子
- **Frank Jr. / Alice**: Phoebe 的弟弟和弟媳，私奔结婚，请 Phoebe 代孕
- **surrogate**: 代孕妈妈，Phoebe 决定替 Frank 和 Alice 怀孕
- **Kathy**: Chandler 的新女友，Joey 的前女友，让 Chandler 有心理压力
- **the museum**: Ross 工作的自然历史博物馆，Joey 在这里当导游
- **blue blazers / white coats**: 蓝西装（导游）和白大褂（科学家），博物馆的阶层象征
- **the Mastodon**: 乳齿象，Joey 说错了它的年代
- **Jurassic / Cretaceous period**: 侏罗纪/白垩纪，Joey 分不清
- **Sophie's Choice**: 电影《苏菲的选择》，比喻极其痛苦的取舍
- **Carl Mulden**: Phoebe 给临时寄养小狗取的名字
