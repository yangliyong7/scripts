# Friends S04E03 口语表达笔记

## 1. 情绪与反应

- **How desperate am I?**: 我有多绝望啊？（自嘲语气，问朋友自己穿成这样是不是很 desperate）
  - *Example*: How desperate am I?
- **Could we BE more white trash?**: 我们还能更乡下人吗？（Chandler 经典句式 Could X BE more Y，自嘲生活方式寒酸）
  - *Example*: Could we be more white trash?
- **How's the hired help?**: 雇来的帮手怎么样啊？（居高临下的调侃，Mrs. Geller 对 Monica 说）
  - *Example*: How's the hired help?
- **I'm out.**: 我答不上来了/我认输。（口语：被问倒时表示放弃）
  - *Example*: He cut off his ear. (The Salesman: And?) I'm out.
- **Oh good, I'm glad that's catching on.**: 哦太好了，这个说法开始流行了啊。（讽刺语气，catching on = 流行起来）
  - *Example*: Oh good, I'm glad that's catching on.
- **My Mom doesn't have any faith in me! Oh, that's hilarious!**: 我妈对我一点信心都没有！哦，这太好笑了！（讽刺式大笑，Monica 被妈妈的冷冻千层面彻底伤到了）
  - *Example*: That's right. My Mom doesn't have any faith in me! Oh, that's hilarious! Ha-ha-ha-ha-ha.
- **I can't believe I actually thought she could change.**: 我不敢相信我居然以为她能改变。（对某人彻底失望）
  - *Example*: Ohh, I'm such an idiot. I can't believe I actually thought she could change.
- **Yes!!**: 太棒了！！（Monica 的招牌式胜利欢呼，夸张到 Phoebe 都吓了一跳）
  - *Example*: Yes!!
- **It's hard to believe that just a little while ago this was nothing but ingredients.**: 真不敢相信刚才还只是一堆食材。（Phoebe 式感叹，夸张表达惊喜）
  - *Example*: It's hard to believe that just a little while ago this was nothing but ingredients.

## 2. 决策与行动

- **take the relationship to the next level**: 把关系推进一步（从约会到亲密关系，或泛指关系升级）
  - *Example*: I think I judged her too quickly, and this time we were able to take the relationship to the next level.
- **get rid of that last little schmidgen of self-respect**: 丢掉最后那一点点自尊（schmidgen 是编造词，幽默用法，表达自嘲）
  - *Example*: I thought that it'd be a great way to get rid of that last little schmidgen of self-respect.
- **I judged her too quickly**: 我对她判断得太快了（承认自己看走眼了）
  - *Example*: Well, I think I judged her too quickly.
- **prove your Mother wrong**: 证明你妈是错的
  - *Example*: Go down there and prove your Mother wrong. Finish the job you were hired to do.
- **you got yourself into those cuffs, you get yourself out of them**: 你自己把自己铐上的，你自己想办法解开（自作自受的意思）
  - *Example*: Y'know what Chandler, you got yourself into those cuffs, you get yourself out of them.
- **I'll make something up**: 我来编个理由（make up = 编造、虚构）
  - *Example*: I'll make something up! I'm good at lying, I actually did borrow your Walkman!
- **You don't have to stick up for her.**: 你不用替她说话。（stick up for = 替……辩护/说好话）
  - *Example*: You don't have to stick up for her. She can't here you.
- **What can you swing?**: 你能出多少？（swing = 负担得起、拿得出）
  - *Example*: Well ah, what can you swing?

## 3. 社交与关系

- **end it**: 结束（一段关系/分手）
  - *Example*: You must end it, you must end it now!
- **you're not the boss of me**: 你管不着我（俏皮拒绝，像小孩子说的话）
  - *Example*: Ah-ha, you're not the boss of me. (She kisses him) Yeah, you are!
- **I did break up with her! She just took it really, really well!**: 我确实跟她分了！只是她接受得特别好！（讽刺：Joanna 根本没当回事，分手后直接上床了）
  - *Example*: I did break up with her! She just took it really, really well!
- **make you a legend**: 把你变成传奇人物
  - *Example*: I can make you a legend. I can make you this generation's Milton Berle.
- **we're definately broken up this time**: 这次我们铁定分了（definately 是剧本故意拼错，模仿口语发音）
  - *Example*: You tell your friend Chandler that we're definately broken up this time.
- **It doesn't feel like we're breaking up.**: 感觉不像在分手啊。（Chandler 被铐在椅子上还在亲热的讽刺场景）
  - *Example*: It just doesn't…feel like we're breaking up.
- **never entered your mind?!**: 你脑子里完全没想过吗？！（enter one's mind = 想到/闪过脑海）
  - *Example*: And the fact that you were jeopardising my career never entered your mind?!

## 4. 描述事物

- **white trash**: 白人底层阶级（自嘲用语，指生活方式寒酸、品味低下）
  - *Example*: Could we be more white trash?
- **all this rusty crap for free**: 这一堆生锈的破烂白送的（crap = 垃圾/破烂，口语贬义）
  - *Example*: Wow! Really?! We get all this rusty crap for free?!
- **pulled a Monica**: 搞砸了/Monica 式的失误（本集发明的说法，后来 Phoebe 试图重新定义为"做到了不起的事"）
  - *Example*: This was just in case you pulled a Monica.
- **vulcanised rubber**: 硫化橡胶（Joey 以为是 Spock 的避孕措施，因为他只知道 Star Trek 里的 Spock）
  - *Example*: What do you know about vulcanised rubber? — Spock's birth control.
- **a violent igneous rock formation**: 一种暴烈的火成岩构造（Joey 从百科全书 V 册学到的，现学现卖）
  - *Example*: Yeah. And speaking of volcanoes, man are they a violent igneous rock formation.
- **a baby Tootsie Roll, a movie stub, keys, a Kleenex, a rock, and an army man**: 一小根太妃卷糖、一张电影票根、钥匙、纸巾、一块石头和一个玩具兵（Joey 口袋里的全部家当，体现他的幼稚和穷）
  - *Example*: I've got a baby Tootsie Roll, a movie stub, keys, a Kleenex, a rock, and an army man.
- **I got patio furniture in my living room**: 我客厅里摆的是户外家具（暗示自己穷到买不起正经家具）
  - *Example*: I'm home in the middle of the day, and I got patio furniture in my living room.
- **some are dormant**: 有些是休眠的（指休眠火山，Joey 卖弄新学的知识）
  - *Example*: Oh yeah, lava spewing, hot ash, of course some are dormant.
- **extra pulp**: 多果肉的（橙汁里的果肉，Chandler 故意挑 Rachel 承诺里他最讨厌的东西来拒绝）
  - *Example*: With extra pulp?

## 5. 实用短句

- **Wait right there.**: 在这等着别动。
  - *Example*: Treeger said that we could have all this cool stuff from the basement. Wait right there.
- **Just pickle.**: 只有腌黄瓜汁。（简洁到荒谬的回答，Joey 式的极简回复）
  - *Example*: Hey! Umm, do you guys have any juice? — Just pickle.
- **How come?**: 为什么？/ 怎么回事？（口语高频，比 why 更随意）
  - *Example*: I just told my Mom I'd cater a party for her. — How come?
- **Funny story.**: 说来话长/有个好玩的事。（引出话题的口语开场白）
  - *Example*: Hey uh, Rach, funny story. I ah, bumped into Joanna on the street yesterday.
- **Are you the decision maker of the house?**: 你是这家的当家人吗？（推销员的标准开场白）
  - *Example*: Good afternoon, are you the decision maker of the house?
- **I guess there's a few things you don't get from book learnin'.**: 看来有些事光靠书本是学不到的。（Joey 自嘲没钱买书，book learnin' 是故意模仿乡下口音）
  - *Example*: Twelve hundred dollars? You think I have $1200? I'm home in the middle of the day, and I got patio furniture in my living room. I guess there's a few things you don't get from book learnin'.
- **I get the picture.**: 我明白了/我懂了。（picture = 全貌/情况，口语常用）
  - *Example*: Okay, I-I get the picture. Uh, thanks, for your time.
- **These must be Chandler's pants.**: 这肯定是 Chandler 的裤子。（Joey 在自己口袋里翻出 50 美元，幽默推给 Chandler）
  - *Example*: And a 50. Huh, these must be Chandler's pants.
- **Go on.**: 继续说。（表示感兴趣、让对方往下说）
  - *Example*: (intrigued) Go on.
- **Though some things don't change.**: 不过有些事是不会变的。（Phoebe 幽默自嘲：她举了一堆"pull a Monica"的好例子，最后承认有些用法改不了）
  - *Example*: "Yeah, that one's outta here." Though some things don't change.

## 6. 剧情词汇

- **Joanna**: Rachel 在 Bloomingdale's 的上司，Chandler 的炮友对象，本集核心冲突制造者
- **Sophie**: Joanna 办公室的另一位员工，和 Rachel 一起撞见被铐住的 Chandler
- **Mrs. Geller**: Monica 和 Ross 的妈妈，请 Monica 来办派对但对她毫无信心
- **Dr. Weinburg**: Mrs. Geller 的心理治疗师，她曾答应不再用"pull a Monica"这个说法
- **The Salesman**: 上门推销百科全书的推销员，成功卖给 Joey 一本 V 册
- **Van Gogh**: 荷兰画家梵高，Joey 只知道他割了耳朵，不知道他画得好
- **Vatican City**: 梵蒂冈城，教皇的居住地，Joey 以为教皇住在树林里
- **Spock**: 《星际迷航》中的瓦肯人角色，Joey 把他和硫化橡胶联系到一起
- **Milton Berle**: 美国喜剧演员，以大胆露骨的表演闻名，Rachel 拿来类比 Chandler 的"壮举"
- **Vesuvius**: 维苏威火山（摧毁庞贝的火山），Joey 用百科全书学到的词
- **Korean War**: 朝鲜战争，大家讨论时 Joey 又接不上话了
- **Algonquin kids table**: Algonquin Round Table 是 1920 年代纽约文人社交圈，Chandler 用它比喻牌桌上的人很知识分子
- **quiche**: 法式咸派，Monica 为妈妈的派对准备的食物，假指甲掉进去了
- **frozen lasagna**: 冷冻千层面，Mrs. Geller 的后备方案，证明她对 Monica 没信心
- **Walkman**: 索尼随身听，Rachel 要求 Chandler 赔她一个
- **Tootsie Roll**: 美国巧克力太妃糖品牌，Joey 口袋里的零食
- **the chick and the duck**: Joey 和 Chandler 的宠物鸡和鸭，本集中鸭子去 bathroom 的场景很搞笑
- **handcuffs**: 手铐，本集标题中 'Cuffs 的来源，也是 Chandler 被困的核心道具
