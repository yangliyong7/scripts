# Friends S03E24 口语表达笔记

## 1. 情绪与反应

- **What's wrong with me?!**: 我到底怎么了？（情绪激动时质问自己）
  - *Example*: Why? Why?! What's wrong with me?!
- **What's the matter?**: 怎么了？／出什么事了？（关心对方的常用问法）
  - *Example*: What's the matter?
- **Forget it.**: 算了。（因被打断或受挫而放弃继续说）
  - *Example*: Forget it.
- **I have no idea.**: 我完全不知道。（也可表示被打断后忘了原本要说什么）
  - *Example*: I have no idea.
- **That was depressing.**: 那真让人沮丧。（评价刚经历的事情）
  - *Example*: Well that was depressing, I think I just bought a soft pretzel from one of the kids from *Fame*.
- **Oh my God!**: 天哪！（表达震惊）
  - *Example*: Oh my God! Phoebe look, it's Ross and that girl.
- **You are insane!**: 你疯了！（强烈反对对方的危险决定）
  - *Example*: You are insane! You-you gotta give this up!
- **I can't watch this.**: 我看不下去了。（面对令人紧张或难受的场面）
  - *Example*: Oh, I can't watch this.

## 2. 决策与行动

- **keep on doing something**: 继续做某事。（强调动作不要停）
  - *Example*: Keep on scooching.
- **set someone up on a date**: 给某人安排相亲／约会。
  - *Example*: Okay, you can totally say no, but umm, would it be okay with you if I set Ross up on a date?
- **be still on for tonight**: 今晚的约定仍然照常。
  - *Example*: Hey, are we still on for tonight?
- **from now on**: 从现在开始。
  - *Example*: All right so, Chandler, from now on, don't give your boss a chance to get you.
- **look back with no regrets**: 回首时不留遗憾。
  - *Example*: And now you can just look back at this thing with no regrets.
- **if all goes as planned**: 如果一切按计划进行。
  - *Example*: But, it's gonna be okay, because as of tomorrow I'm conducting an experiment, and if all goes as planned, my butt will be smack free.
- **give something up**: 放弃某件事。
  - *Example*: You are insane! You-you gotta give this up!
- **I've got to do this.**: 我必须这么做。（表达坚定决心）
  - *Example*: I've got to do this.

## 3. 社交与关系

- **no longer my friend**: 不再是我的朋友。（正式而强烈地宣布断交）
  - *Example*: And you are no longer my friend!
- **keep your name**: 婚后保留自己的姓氏。
  - *Example*: No-no-no oh, keep your name, don't take his name.
- **good to have you aboard**: 欢迎你加入团队。（职场常用表达）
  - *Example*: Good to have you aboard Bing!
- **you're his guy**: 你是他最器重／最信任的人。（非正式说法）
  - *Example*: With him? You're is favourite, you're his guy!
- **let someone down easy**: 委婉地拒绝某人，尽量不伤对方。
  - *Example*: Uff, I hope he lets her down easy.
- **break them up**: 拆散他们。
  - *Example*: We have to break them up!
- **you owe me one**: 你欠我一次／你得补偿我。
  - *Example*: You owe me one bald girl!!
- **feel left out**: 感觉被冷落、被排除在外。
  - *Example*: You're not feeling left out or anything are ya?

## 4. 描述事物

- **sorta like**: 有点像……（非正式口语，等于 *sort of like*）
  - *Example*: It's-it's sorta like wrestling.
- **anything goes**: 什么都可以／几乎没有限制。
  - *Example*: Yeah, anything goes, except ah, eye gouging and fish hooking.
- **no big deal**: 没什么大不了的。
  - *Example*: Today, my boss keep slapping my butt and he was acting like it was no big deal.
- **average height, medium build**: 中等身高、中等身材。（描述外貌的常用搭配）
  - *Example*: She's yeah, like, average height, medium build, bald...
- **in serious, serious trouble**: 麻烦大了。（重复 *serious* 加强语气）
  - *Example*: Well, then that guy is in serious, serious trouble.
- **not as bad as it looks**: 没看起来那么糟。
  - *Example*: It's okay, it's not as bad as it looks, it's a precaution.
- **a rather sensitive posterior**: 相当敏感的臀部。（Chandler 故意用正式词制造幽默）
  - *Example*: It's just that I, I have a rather, sensitive posterior, and ah, besides, it's making all the other guys jealous.
- **feel someone out**: 试探某人，摸清对方的实力或态度。
  - *Example*: It looks like, he's just trying to feel him out.

## 5. 实用短句

- **Do you mind if we...?**: 你介意我们……吗？（礼貌请求）
  - *Example*: Do you mind if we...
- **Could you pass me...?**: 你能把……递给我吗？
  - *Example*: Could you pass me the cream?
- **You can totally say no.**: 你完全可以拒绝。（提出请求前减轻对方压力）
  - *Example*: Okay, you can totally say no, but umm, would it be okay with you if I set Ross up on a date?
- **Would it be okay with you if...?**: 如果……你介意吗？（委婉征求许可）
  - *Example*: Okay, you can totally say no, but umm, would it be okay with you if I set Ross up on a date?
- **Promise me you'll be careful.**: 答应我你会小心。
  - *Example*: Promise me you'll be careful.
- **I gotta tell you...**: 我得告诉你……（口语中用于引出看法或信息）
  - *Example*: I gotta tell you, I-I wasn't expecting to like her at all, I mean I actually wasn't expecting to like anyone right now, but she's really terrific.
- **Don't get me wrong.**: 别误会我的意思。
  - *Example*: Oh, and don't get me wrong, I appreciate the sentiment.
- **Say no more.**: 不必再解释了／我明白了。
  - *Example*: Well, say no more.
- **I don't get it.**: 我不明白。
  - *Example*: Second of all, I don't get it.
- **Please tell me...**: 请告诉我……（常用于迫切希望得到某种答案）
  - *Example*: Please tell me you're stopping now.

## 6. 剧情词汇

- **Ultimate Fighting Championship**: Pete 执意参加的综合格斗赛事，也是 Monica 与他分手的直接原因。
- **Pete Becker**: Monica 的富豪男友；他把成为终极格斗冠军当成必须完成的人生目标。
- **Tank Abbott**: Pete 的首场对手，体型和实战能力远胜 Pete。
- **Hoshi**: Pete 的训练师；一边训练 Pete，一边抱怨 Pete 公司的电脑客服。
- **Doug**: Chandler 的新上司，以拍下属屁股表达认可，引出办公室边界问题。
- **Bonnie**: Phoebe 介绍给 Ross 的约会对象；Rachel 原以为她仍是光头，见到她长出头发后立刻后悔。
- **Billy Crystal and Robin Williams**: 冷开场客串明星；两人的即兴争吵完全盖过 Monica 原本要讲的消息。
- ***City Slickers***: Billy Crystal 主演的电影，剧本用它帮助观众识别客串演员。
- ***Mrs. Doubtfire***: Robin Williams 主演的电影，同样用于点明客串身份。
- ***Fame***: 以纽约表演艺术学生为主题的影视作品；Rachel 用它嘲讽街头卖椒盐卷饼的年轻人。
- **Moss 865**: Pete 成功开发的软件；他用前 864 次失败为继续格斗辩护。
- **fish hooking**: 格斗中把手指伸进对手嘴里拉扯脸颊的犯规动作，Joey 直接拿 Ross 演示。
- **Dartmouth**: 美国常春藤盟校；Doug 随口贬低它，让需要供孩子上学的 Stevens 更想得到老板认可。
- **Tapanzi Bridge**: Doug 酒后驾车笑话中提到的桥名，原剧本如此拼写。
- **Richard**: Monica 的前男友；结尾 Ross 用他“不想要孩子”与 Pete 遭受的伤势制造黑色幽默。
