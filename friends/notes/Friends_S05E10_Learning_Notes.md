# Friends S05E10 口语表达笔记

## 1. 情绪与反应

- **Out of my mind**: 无聊到发疯。
  - *Example*: Because I am bored…**Out of my mind**.
- **For the love of God, leave me alone**: 看在上帝份上别烦我（极度抓狂）。
  - *Example*: **For the love of God, leave me alone**!!!
- **It's been six months! It's not funny!**: 都半年了还不好笑！（Chandler 崩溃）。
  - *Example*: **It's been six months! It's not funny!**
- **What the hell was that?**: 刚才那算什么？！
  - *Example*: Okay. **What the hell was that?**
- **Bite me, blondie!**: 去你的，金发妞！（路人回怼 Phoebe）。
  - *Example*: **Bite me, blondie!**
- **They're brother and sister!!!**: 他们真是兄妹！！！（Joey 震惊复读）。
  - *Example*: **They're brother and sister!!!**
- **I don't believe they're brother and sister**: 我不相信他们是兄妹（Chandler）。
  - *Example*: **I don't believe they're brother and sister**.

## 2. 决策与行动

- **Reorganized the fridge**: 重新整理冰箱（Ross 无聊时的「项目」）。
  - *Example*: I…**reorganized the fridge**. See, bottom shelf: meats and dairy.
- **Spread it out**: 把事摊开做，别一天做完。
  - *Example*: You've got to **spread it out** a little.
- **Make something happen for yourself**: 自己找机会、自己闯（Ross 劝 Joey 写剧本）。
  - *Example*: You should **make something happen for yourself**. Write a play. Write a movie!
- **Stick to his schedule**: 按计划表写作（Ross 监督 Joey）。
  - *Example*: I have to write five whole pages if I'm gonna **stick to his schedule**.
- **Crank it up a notch**: 再加点料、再刺激一点。
  - *Example*: How about we **crank it up a notch**?
- **Unhook the smoke detectors**: 拆掉烟雾报警器（玩 Fireball 前奏）。
  - *Example*: I'll go **unhook the smoke detectors**!
- **Tore up the tickets**: 撕了票（Ross 赌气撕冰球票）。
  - *Example*: There! Now, no one's going to the game. *(He **rips up the tickets**.)*
- **Settle this over a friendly game**: 用友好比赛解决争端。
  - *Example*: How about we **settle this over a friendly game of Fireball**?

## 3. 社交与关系

- **I'm on sabbatical**: 我在学术休假（Ross 坚决否认 unemployed）。
  - *Example*: Hey, I am not unemployed. **I'm on sabbatical**!
- **Don't get religious on me**: 别跟我扯宗教（Joey 把 sabbatical 听成双关）。
  - *Example*: Hey, **don't get religious on me**, ok.
- **Pretend like we don't know him**: 假装不认识他（欲擒故纵）。
  - *Example*: Just **pretend like we don't know him**. We've forgotten who he is.
- **Do you want to go out on a date with her?**: Monica 当面牵线，Rachel 抓狂。
  - *Example*: **Do you want to go out on a date with her?** — **Monica!!!**
- **You meddled in our relationship**: 你搅和我们的「关系」（Rachel 怪 Monica）。
  - *Example*: You **meddled in our relationship**!!
- **According to the plan**: 按计划进行（Rachel 自以为的暗恋策略）。
  - *Example*: Everything was going **according to the plan**!
- **Stop with the plan!**: 别再说什么计划了！（Monica）。
  - *Example*: Oh God, **stop with the plan**!
- **Special bond**: 特别亲密的关系（Danny 抱怨女生都误会他和妹妹）。
  - *Example*: Oh great! That **special bond** again!
- **Stand in the way of us**: 妨碍我们在一起（Danny 质问 Rachel）。
  - *Example*: Are you gonna let that **stand in the way of us**?

## 4. 描述事物

- **Seven days worth of stuff**: 够撑七天的事（Joey 吐槽 Ross 一天干完一周 errands）。
  - *Example*: Dude, you just described **seven days worth of stuff**.
- **Comfortable chairs / foot rest**: 躺椅脚凳（Joey 教 Ross 放松）。
  - *Example*: Why do you think we have the **comfortable chairs**? *(Joey flips the **foot rest** up.)*
- **Collection donations**: 募捐（Phoebe 圣诞当铃铛志愿者）。
  - *Example*: I'm going to be one of those people **collection donations**.
- **Spreading joy**: 传播欢乐（往桶里投钱后挥手比划）。
  - *Example*: I'm going to be out there **spreading joy** to the people.
- **Nobody respects the bucket**: 没人尊重募捐桶（被当垃圾桶/烟灰缸/尿壶）。
  - *Example*: **Nobody respects the bucket**!
- **No more Mrs. Nice Bucket**: 不再当好说话的桶小姐（Phoebe 变硬气）。
  - *Example*: No more **Mrs. Nice Bucket**!
- **Street Phoebe**: 街头版 Phoebe（她扬言要回归）。
  - *Example*: I'm gonna go back to being **Street Phoebe**.
- **Wrestley**: 像摔跤一样（Rachel 形容兄妹互动）。
  - *Example*: They were very y'know…**wrestley**.
- **Quick as a cat**: 像猫一样敏捷（Monica 自称小时候摔跤厉害）。
  - *Example*: Still, I was **quick as a cat**.
- **Inappropriate sister**: 举止过火的妹妹（本集标题梗 Krista）。
  - *Example*: The **Inappropriate Sister** — Krista 与 Danny 过分亲密。
- **Hammer darts / Ultimate Fireball**: 锤子飞镖；终极 Fireball（保龄球+丙烷）。
  - *Example*: We invented **hammer darts**. / …coming up with new, **Ultimate Fireball**.
- **Masculine energy**: 很有男性气概（Rachel 形容某个姐姐）。
  - *Example*: One of them has a very **masculine energy**.

## 5. 实用短句

- **The British are coming?**: 英国人要来了？→对「猜个事儿」的冷幽默（保罗·里维尔梗）。
  - *Example*: **The British are coming?** — Ohh, you and your ways.
- **And that's Wednesday**: 这就是周三版→每周恶作剧变体（今天周三版）。
  - *Example*: **And that's Wednesday.** *(He reclines in his chair.)*
- **I love you** (prank call): 假扮女声说「我爱你」→电话恶作剧经典台词。
  - *Example*: Hello Mr. Bing…**I love you**.
- **Chasing the Churro guy isn't jogging**: 追吉拿棒小贩不算慢跑→戳穿 Joey 编的「公园慢跑」。
  - *Example*: First off all, **chasing the Churro guy isn't jogging**.
- **Breathing down your neck ALL THE LIVE LONG DAY**: 整天盯着你脖子→无时无刻管着你（live long=整个）。
  - *Example*: Somebody **breathing down your neck ALL THE LIVE LONG DAY**!!
- **Cut him a little slack**: 放他一马→别逼太紧。
  - *Example*: Why don't you **cut him a little slack**? Okay?
- **Give them a project, ruin their day**: 给孩子找事干、毁他们一天→讽刺对方爱控制人。
  - *Example*: Wanna go down there and **give them a project, ruin their day**?
- **Gonna Need Stitches Ball**: 需要缝针球→Chandler 编的危险游戏名讽刺 Ross。
  - *Example*: Stick razor blades in it and teach them a new game, **Gonna Need Stitches Ball**.
- **That stain's gonna set**: 污渍要固住了→借口上楼脱裤子（set=定型）。
  - *Example*: We'd better take these pants off upstairs or **that stain's gonna set**.
- **The bath is getting cold**: 澡要凉了→Krista 在浴室喊 Danny，Rachel 落荒而逃。
  - *Example*: Danny! Hurry up! **The bath is getting cold**!
- **Hurt bunny look**: 受伤小兔子表情→叫捐款人别摆可怜脸。
  - *Example*: You can leave the **hurt bunny look** over there too!
- **That chick can't handle my corner**: 那女的搞不定我的街角→Phoebe 被 Ginger 取代。
  - *Example*: **That chick can't handle my corner**.
- **Less high-profile spot**: 不那么显眼的位置→Bob 调 Phoebe 岗位。
  - *Example*: We're gonna move you to a **less high-profile spot**.
- **She nailed it**: 她演绝了→抢角成功（Estelle：Talia Shire）。
  - *Example*: What can I say? **She nailed it**.
- **Could I be more sorry**: 我还能更抱歉吗→模仿 Chandler 句型写进剧本。
  - *Example*: **Could I be more sorry**.
- **At heart**: 出于好心→心底里（we had our best friend's interest **at heart**）。
  - *Example*: We had our best friend's interest **at heart**.
- **This took you all day?!**: 这花你一整天？！→其实只写了五分钟。
  - *Example*: **This took you all day?!** — No-no, this only took five minutes.
- **I'll take you right now, buddy!**: 现在就干一架，老兄！（Monica 和 Ross 扭打）。
  - *Example*: **I'll take you right now, buddy!** You wanna go?
- **Feed it to him** (cake on finger): 用手指喂蛋糕（Krista 喂 Danny，众人震惊）。
  - *Example*: She takes some on her finger and **feeds it to** Danny.

## 6. 剧情词汇

- **S05E10 / The Inappropriate Sister**: 本集标题指 Danny 妹妹 Krista 的亲密举止
- **Danny & Krista**: 新邻居 Danny；妹妹 Krista（地铁、公寓、Central Perk）
- **Macy's bell-ringing spot**: Phoebe 募捐街角，后被 Ginger 取代
- **Joey's screenplay**: Ross 监督下的五页写作任务
- **Fireball / Ultimate Fireball**: 网球+碗+打火机油；片尾保龄球加强版
- **NY Rangers game tickets**: Ross 与 Chandler 冲突导火索（撕票）
- **Joey's apology script**: 让 Ross/Chandler 朗读的和好短剧；片尾「两女生」版被扔脸
- **Good Will Hunting guys**: Matt & Ben 梗（Ross 劝 Joey 写剧本）
- **Official rulebook of Fireball**: Joey 实际在写的「作品」
