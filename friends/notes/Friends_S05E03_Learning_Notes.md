# Friends S05E03 口语表达笔记

## 1. 情绪与反应
- **freak people out**: 把人吓到/吓懵（Phoebe 对护士说父亲是哥哥）。
  - *Example*: I am so gonna miss watching you **freak people out** like that!
- **That's easy for you to say**: 你说得倒轻松→你没在经历我的事。
  - *Example*: **That's easy for you to say**, I don't see three kids coming out your vagina!
- **I didn't know I cared that much**: 没想到我这么在乎（Joey 的 sympathy pains）。
  - *Example*: Are they? **I didn't know I cared that much**.
- **I was bummed too**: 我也挺郁闷/失望（男护士约会）。
  - *Example*: Yeah, **I was bummed too**.
- **I'm gonna be on the news!**: 我要上新闻了→夸张惊呼（Phoebe 想留一个孩子）。
  - *Example*: Ohh, **I'm gonna be on the news**!
- **kindergarten flashback**: 幼儿园阴影回闪→名字被当成女孩名的童年创伤。
  - *Example*: Oh God, **kindergarten flashback**.
- **You made him cry!**: 你把他弄哭了！（Frank 反而夸 Phoebe 赶走小医生）。
  - *Example*: Oh cool! **You made him cry**!
- **Are you crazy?!**: 你疯了吗？！（Joey 听说 urethra 治疗方案）。
  - *Example*: **Are you crazy?!**

## 2. 决策与行动
- **get the before shot**: 拍「产前」镜头（Joey 拿摄像机）。
  - *Example*: I gotta **get the before shot**!
- **get over Ross and move on**: 放下 Ross、往前走→开始新约会。
  - *Example*: You're the one who's been telling me to **get over Ross and move on**.
- **get out of it**: 推掉、脱身（Monica 本想取消和 Dan 的约会）。
  - *Example*: I was going to **get out of it**, but hey, if we're just goofing around then maybe I will go out with him.
- **get me another doctor**: 给我换医生。
  - *Example*: Just **get me another doctor**! One who is not crazy and who is not Fonzie!
- **pass them**: pass 排出去→（肾结石）自然排出体外。
  - *Example*: We can either wait for you to **pass them** or else go up the urethra…
- **feel him out**: 试探口风、摸摸态度（Phoebe 让 Rachel 问 Frank）。
  - *Example*: Just talk to Frank. Okay? Just, y'know, **feel him out**!
- **hold them in**: 憋着不生→让胎儿再等等（Frank 荒唐请求）。
  - *Example*: You couldn't just like do me a favor and like, like **hold them in**?
- **cover my shift**: 找人代班（Dan 答应改约会时间）。
  - *Example*: Sure! I'll get somebody to **cover my shift**.
- **Undo it**: 撤回、收回刚才说的（Rachel 提了 Mork，Phoebe 急喊）。
  - *Example*: **Undo it. Undo it. Undo it.**

## 3. 社交与关系
- **sympathy pains**: 连心痛→为别人（尤其伴侣）感同身受的身体疼痛。
  - *Example*: Ooh, it's **sympathy pains**. Ohh, that's so sweet!
- **steal my thunder**: 抢风头、抢注意力（Joey 装疼）。
  - *Example*: Now I think he's just trying to **steal my thunder**.
- **goofin' around**: 玩玩而已、不是认真交往（Chandler 掩饰醋意）。
  - *Example*: You and I we're just, y'know, we're nothing, we're **goofin' around**.
- **inappropriate**: 不合时宜、越界（Phoebe 自己不能开口要孩子）。
  - *Example*: Do you have any idea how **inappropriate** that would be?!
- **Tell him it's for you**: 跟他说是给你要的→让 Rachel 代问（Phoebe 的歪招）。
  - *Example*: **Tell him it's for you**.
- **Know when to stop**: 知道何时收嘴/收手（Monica 打断 Chandler 表白）。
  - *Example*: (interrupting) **Know when to stop**.
- **Don't do the dance**: 别跳那段舞→别得意忘形庆祝（Chandler 想跳舞）。
  - *Example*: Without turning around.) **Don't do the dance**.

## 4. 描述事物
- **in good hands**: 交在可靠的人手里。
  - *Example*: You're gonna be **in good hands**. I've been doing this for a long time.
- **a handful**: 一把抓不过来→很难应付、够呛（三个孩子）。
  - *Example*: Seriously, three babies are **a handful** maybe they're looking for a chance to unload one of them.
- **long shot**: 希望渺茫的事、碰运气（Phoebe 想留一个孩子）。
  - *Example*: Oh. It was a **long shot**.
- **on a clock here**: 时间在倒计时→我这边很急（快生了）。
  - *Example*: Well, I'm kinda **on a clock here**.
- **grown up doctor**: 成年医生→要个像样的、不是小屁孩（讽刺住院医）。
  - *Example*: Ross, maybe I should've specified that I'd be needing a **grown up doctor**.
- **getting there**: 快到位了、有进展了（产程）。
  - *Example*: All right, you're **getting there**.
- **Kinda girlie isn't it?**: 有点娘/阴柔吧？（Chandler 挑衅男护士）。
  - *Example*: So Dan, nurse not a doctor huh? **Kinda girlie isn't it?**
- **put myself through medical school**: 打工挣学费读医学院。
  - *Example*: I'm just doing this to **put myself through medical school**.
- **didn't feel so girlie during the Gulf War**: 海湾战争时可不娘→Dan 反击 Chandler。
  - *Example*: And it **didn't feel so girlie during the Gulf War**.
- **So gross!**: 好恶心（Frank 形容新生儿）。
  - *Example*: How does he look? — **So gross**!

## 5. 实用短句
- **what took you guys so long?**: 你们怎么这么久？（Monica/Chandler 迟到借口）。
  - *Example*: Hey, **what took you guys so long**? Your cab left when ours did!
- **I did it again!**: 我又犯了！→老毛病又犯了（Monica 忘穿夹克借口穿帮）。
  - *Example*: Oh man! **I did it again**!
- **Not in my head**: 在我脑子里不是→我幻想里还是女的（Joey 对男护士）。
  - *Example*: They're male nurses. — **Not in my head**.
- **give me one good reason**: 给我一个正当理由（Rachel 逼 Monica 约会）。
  - *Example*: Come on, **give me one good reason** why you don't wanna go.
- **Harder than it sounds**: 说起来容易做起来难（Monica 让 Rachel 先编借口）。
  - *Example*: **Harder than it sounds**. Isn't it?
- **There's always room for Jell-O**: Jell-O 布丁广告 Slogan「永远有 Jell-O 的空间」。
  - *Example*: Yep! **There's always room for Jell-O**…
- **How do you make that dirty?**: 你怎么把这也说得色情？（Joey 把广告念得很骚）。
  - *Example*: Joey, **how do you make that dirty**?
- **To be fair**: 公平地说、说句公道话。
  - *Example*: **To be fair**, he doesn't seem to be impersonating Fonzie…
- **Up is not an option**: 「往上」不在选项里→绝不允许从尿道进（Joey 拒治）。
  - *Example*: No-no-no-no-no, nothing is going up! Okay? **Up, up is not an option**
- **miss an opportunity just because I didn't ask**: 不能因为没开口就错过机会（Phoebe 哲学）。
  - *Example*: I hate to **miss an opportunity just because I didn't ask**!
- **fair enough**: 好吧、说得通→接受对方观点（Rachel 对 Frank）。
  - *Example*: Not to me. — Yeah, **fair enough**.
- **I want the drugs**: 我要止痛药（Joey 产痛式肾结石）。
  - *Example*: **I want the drugs** Ross, I want the drugs!
- **goofing around** (Chandler 版): 字典里指两个很在乎彼此的朋友、性爱很棒、想多待在一起。
  - *Example*: The technical definition is, two friends who care a lot about each other and have amazing sex and just wanna spend more time together.
- **I am so bad at this**: 我真不擅长这个（表白/谈恋爱）。
  - *Example*: **I am so bad at this**.
- **better than you think you are**: 比你以为的要好（Monica 鼓励 Chandler）。
  - *Example*: I think you're **better than you think you are**.
- **I sensed that I should stop**: 我隐约觉得该停了（Chandler 自知话太多）。
  - *Example*: Y'know, **I sensed that I should stop**. So we're okay?
- **Works on you**: 用在你身上也挺合适→名字中性，你叫 Chandler 也行（Phoebe 怼人）。
  - *Example*: That's kind of a masculine name, don't you think? — **Works on you**.
- **You won't be able to take it**: 你受不了的→分娩疼得你扛不住（Phoebe 吓唬 Rachel）。
  - *Example*: Yeah. **You won't be able to take it**.

## 6. 剧情词汇
- **S05E03 / The One Hundredth**: 全剧第 100 集；Phoebe 生下三胞胎
- **Fonzie / The Fonz**: 《Happy Days》角色，Harad 医生全程痴迷
- **Dr. Harad**: 产科医生，接生时要开电视看 Fonzie
- **Dr. Oberman / Doogie**: 被 Phoebe 当小学生赶走的住院医
- **kidney stones**: Joey 肾结石 subplot，与 sympathy pains 呼应
- **Frank Jr. Jr. / Leslie / Chandler**: 三胞胎；第三个超声误判性别
- **petri dish**: 试管受精，Phoebe 跟婴儿告别时提起
- **Dan**: 男护士，Monica 用来试探 Chandler 的约会对象
- **la-Mazada**: Frank 对 Lamaze 拉梅兹呼吸法的误读
- **Playpen**: 剧中版《Playboy》，Joey 给新生儿准备的「礼物」
