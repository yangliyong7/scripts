# Friends S04E15 口语表达笔记

## 1. 情绪与反应

- **I can't stand the woman**: 我受不了那个女人。
  - *Example*: I can't stand the woman!
- **And there it is**: 果然来了（预料中的事发生，常带调侃）。
  - *Example*: (Janice 发出标志性大笑) Joey: And there it is.
- **You don't say**: 是吗／看不出来（常带讽刺的惊讶）。
  - *Example*: Tell the lads to go easy on Ross, it's his first time. — Liam: You don't say!
- **That's so freaky**: 这也太诡异了。
  - *Example*: Oh my God, that's so freaky! Turn him off!!
- **Saucy**: 调皮、风情万种。
  - *Example*: (Rachel 摸到 Joshua 的脉搏) Monica: Saucy!
- **I think I'm dying**: 我觉得我快死了（夸张喊疼）。
  - *Example*: I-I think I'm dying. I really do.
- **Thank God**: 谢天谢地。
  - *Example*: (Monica 终于放弃研究开关) Rachel: Thank God.

## 2. 决策与行动

- **break up with her**: 跟她分手。
  - *Example*: You have never been able to break up with her.
- **do a pre-emptive strike**: 先发制人（字面"提前打击"，指抢先下手掐掉苗头）。
  - *Example*: I'm going to do a pre-emptive strike! I'm going to end it with her before it starts.
- **I'm taking care of it tonight**: 我今晚会把它搞定。
  - *Example*: Don't worry about it. I'm taking care of it tonight.
- **get rid of her**: 摆脱她、甩掉她。
  - *Example*: I'm only going to pretend I'm moving to Yemen, it's the only way I can get rid of her.
- **I've been meaning to ring you**: 我一直想给你打电话（mean to do＝打算；ring＝英式"打电话"）。
  - *Example*: I've been meaning to ring you ever since I arrived.
- **go easy on him**: 对他手下留情。
  - *Example*: Tell the lads to go easy on Ross, it's his first time.
- **I insist on finishing this game**: 我坚持要打完这场比赛。
  - *Example*: I don't care! I am not quitting! I insist on finishing this game!
- **back off**: 退缩、住手。
  - *Example*: One swift kick and he'll back off.
- **I officially give up**: 我正式放弃。
  - *Example*: I officially give up.

## 3. 社交与关系

- **crazy about her**: 对她着迷、很喜欢她。
  - *Example*: I thought you were crazy about her!
- **riding the alimony pony**: 靠前夫的赡养费过日子（alimony＝离婚后付的钱，这里是俏皮说法）。
  - *Example*: Yeah, I'm riding the alimony pony.
- **This doesn't make you any less of a guy**: 这不会让你少了男子气概。
  - *Example*: Chandler, don't worry! This doesn't make you any less of a guy!
- **I am not letting you get away this time**: 这次我不会再放你跑掉。
  - *Example*: Face it honey, I am not letting you get away this time.
- **meant to be**: 天生一对、命中注定。
  - *Example*: And I'm just getting out of a marriage, I mean talk about meant to be!
- **trade lives with me**: 跟我交换人生吧（Chandler 求 Joey 替他承受 Janice）。
  - *Example*: Joey, trade lives with me! — Joey: Nope.
- **say hello to Mrs. Chatracus**: 跟 Chatracus 太太打个招呼。
  - *Example*: Say hello to Mrs. Chatracus.

## 4. 描述事物

- **man enough to**: 够爷们、有胆量去……
  - *Example*: I'm man enough to play this sport. / You're not even man enough to order the channel.
- **drive someone crazy**: 让某人抓狂。
  - *Example*: Didn't it drive you crazy to have a switch and not know what it did?
- **a trick hip**: 有毛病、不灵光的髋关节（trick＝靠不住的、容易出问题的）。
  - *Example*: And that big bloke with the beard, he has got a trick hip.
- **go down like a lamp**: 一碰就倒（像关灯一样"啪"地倒下）。
  - *Example*: Liam's got bad knees. You hit him right and he'll go down like a lamp.
- **soak up every ounce of**: 尽情吸收、珍惜每一分。
  - *Example*: I will just have to soak up every ounce of Chandler Bing until that moment comes.
- **not to be outdone**: 不甘示弱。
  - *Example*: ...not to be outdone, Ross crushes his coffee cup, spilling its contents.
- **The wires have come loose in your head**: 你脑子里的电线松了（骂人脑子短路、疯了）。
  - *Example*: I don't care! The wires have come loose in your head!

## 5. 实用短句

- **too soon, too schmoon**: "快什么快"（用谐音押韵回怼对方的话，表示不以为然）。
  - *Example*: Oh, too soon, too schmoon. Face it honey, I am not letting you get away this time.
- **talk about...**: 这才叫……／真是……（强调）。
  - *Example*: I mean talk about meant to be!
- **I don't know what the big deal is**: 我不懂这有什么大不了的。
  - *Example*: I'm watching this rugby thing on ESPN. I don't know what the big deal is.
- **No offence, but...**: 别介意，但是……
  - *Example*: No offence but, y'know sometimes it's hard to understand you, y'know with the accent.
- **to start with**: 首先、第一点。
  - *Example*: Well I mean, you're American to start with.
- **Cheers**: （英式）谢谢／再见。
  - *Example*: We'll see you at Riverside Park at 2:00! Cheers!
- **good one**: 好主意、真有你的。
  - *Example*: Ohhhh, good one! And Yemen, that actually sounds like a real country.
- **I see how you did that**: 我看懂你怎么玩的梗了。
  - *Example*: You're kidding! And he plays rugby?! ...Ohh! I see how you did that.
- **I hear ya**: 我懂你、我听到了（ya＝you）。
  - *Example*: I hear ya. (Pause) But!
- **I just lost it**: 我当时一下失控了（lose it＝情绪爆发）。
  - *Example*: ...that one guy cut in line in front of us and I just lost it?!

## 6. 剧情词汇

- **Janice**: Chandler 的前女友，标志性大笑和 "Oh my God"，本集想跟 Chandler 复合
- **rugby**: 英式橄榄球，Ross 为向 Emily 证明自己很爷们而去打
- **Yemen**: 也门，Chandler 谎称被公司调去那里，好甩掉 Janice
- **Red Ross**: Ross 的暴走形态（气到脸红、彻底爆发）
- **the alimony pony**: 赡养费（alimony），Janice 靠离婚赡养费生活
- **scrum**: 英式橄榄球的争球／列阵（Joey 把它比作美式的 huddle）
- **Liam / Devon**: Emily 的英国朋友、橄榄球伙伴
- **Mrs. Chatracus**: Monica 顺着电线挖墙洞后遇到的邻居老太太
- **Old Man River**: 音乐剧《演艺船》的名曲，Janice 在台上唱过
- **Dances With Wolves**: 电影《与狼共舞》，Ross 解释 "Red Ross" 排队被插队暴走的由来
