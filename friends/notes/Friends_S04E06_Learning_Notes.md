# Friends S04E06 口语表达笔记

## 1. 情绪与反应

- **come to papa**: 来吧宝贝/到爸爸这来（得意洋洋的口语，看到好事发生）
  - *Example*: Yeah, come to papa.
- **Fine by me; hope she wins**: 我无所谓/正合我意；希望她赢（自嘲式接受嘲笑）
  - *Example*: Fine by me; hope she wins.
- **What is with all the negativity?**: 干嘛这么消极/负能量爆棚？
  - *Example*: Wow, what is with all the negativity? You sound like Monican't, not Monican...Monica.
- **There you go, that's the spirit!**: 这就对了/这才是精神！（鼓励对方积极起来）
  - *Example*: There you go, that's the spirit! Okay! Now, if you need money, I will lend you money.
- **I'll have one, please. Plus my money**: 我要一个，谢谢，顺便把我的钱还我（幽默催债）
  - *Example*: I'll have one, please. Plus my money.
- **Sorry I acted like a bank**: 抱歉我搞得像个银行一样（自嘲借钱给朋友太爽快）
  - *Example*: Oh. Okay. Oo, sorry I acted like a bank.
- **She seems fine now**: 她看起来现在挺好的嘛（讽刺：刚才还在哭，转眼就唱歌了）
  - *Example*: She seems fine now.
- **I must have freaked out**: 我一定是吓疯了/失控了（freak out = 惊慌失措）
  - *Example*: Oh my god! I'm so sorry, Cheryl. I must have freaked out.
- **I'm a hard ass / I'm a wuss**: 我是个狠角色 / 我是个怂包（hard ass = 强硬的人，wuss = 胆小鬼）
  - *Example*: Yeah. I'm a hard ass. / And I'm a wuss. And we should be partners.
- **Enough is enough!**: 够了就是够了/忍无可忍了！
  - *Example*: No! Hey, we're not leaving until we get paid! I don't know who she thinks she is! Enough is enough!

## 2. 决策与行动

- **I have a call in about that**: 我已经打电话追问了（call in = 打电话询问/催办）
  - *Example*: Well, I have a call in about that.
- **get moving**: 行动起来/赶紧干（催促某人别再拖延）
  - *Example*: Now, if you need money, I will lend you money, but just get moving!
- **turn down a job**: 推掉一个活儿/拒绝一份工作
  - *Example*: I just had to turn down a job catering a funeral for sixty people.
- **on such short notice**: 这么短的时间内/临时通知（notice = 提前通知的时间）
  - *Example*: Because I don't have the money or the equipment to handle something that big on such short notice.
- **settle the bill**: 结账/结算账单
  - *Example*: Yes. Did you settle the bill?
- **try to get out of paying**: 想方设法赖账/逃避付款
  - *Example*: There are some people who just always try to get out of paying.
- **Made to order**: 按订单现做/定制（餐饮业用语）
  - *Example*: I'm an omelet station! Omelet? Made to order!
- **I did it all by myself!**: 我全靠自己搞定了！（强调独立完成）
  - *Example*: I did it! Oh! I finished it! I did it all by myself! And there's nobody to hug!
- **think of us for your next event**: 下次活动记得找我们（推销话术）
  - *Example*: I'm gonna leave some cards here. Please think of us for you next event.
- **mustering up courage**: 鼓起勇气
  - *Example*: (mustering up courage) Yes. Yes I do.
- **spent the entire afternoon looking for**: 花了整个下午去找
  - *Example*: I just spent the entire afternoon looking for a present for Kathy that would be better than the rabbit.

## 3. 社交与关系

- **what a great way to say, "I secretly love you, roommate's girlfriend!"**: 这简直是说"我暗恋你，室友的女朋友！"的好方式（Phoebe 的毒舌吐槽）
  - *Example*: Yeah, and what a great way to say, "I secretly love you, roommate's girlfriend!"
- **do you think I gotta get her something?**: 你觉得我必须送她礼物吗？（gotta = got to，口语"必须"）
  - *Example*: We've only been going out for a couple of weeks, do you think I gotta get her something?
- **not one of your coupons for an hour of "Joey Love"**: 别再搞你那种"Joey之爱一小时"优惠券了
  - *Example*: And not one of your coupons for an hour of "Joey Love."
- **I want her to have it, even if I don't get to be the one who gives it to her**: 我想让她拥有它，就算不是我亲手送的（暗恋的无私）
  - *Example*: The bottom line is I want her to have it, even if I don't get to be the one who gives it to her.
- **You don't think it's just pathetic?**: 你不觉得这很可悲吗？
  - *Example*: Yeah? You don't think it's just pathetic?
- **We should be partners**: 我们应该合伙/搭档
  - *Example*: Wait a minute, Phoebe! We should be partners. We should be catering partners.
- **I can cook and you can take care of the money**: 我负责做饭你负责管钱（分工合作）
  - *Example*: I can cook and you can take care of the money.
- **It'll be like I have a wife in the fifties!**: 就像五十年代有个老婆一样！（Phoebe 式幽默）
  - *Example*: Yeah. Oh! It'll be like I have a wife in the fifties!
- **You must really like Joey to go to all that trouble for him**: 你一定很喜欢Joey才会为他费这么大劲（go to all that trouble = 费尽心思）
  - *Example*: You must really like... Joey... to go to all that trouble for him.
- **I feel kinda bad taking credit for this**: 把功劳揽到自己身上有点不好意思
  - *Example*: Hey, listen, I gotta tell ya, I feel kinda bad taking credit for this, because man, am I gonna get a lot of credit for this!
- **that coupon expires, you know**: 那张优惠券可是有过期日期的（Joey 的搞笑催促）
  - *Example*: Hey, that coupon expires, you know.

## 4. 描述事物

- **the biggest geek**: 最大的书呆子/极客（朋友间调侃的用语）
  - *Example*: Well maybe she and her friends are just having a contest to see who can bring home the biggest geek.
- **have a weird smell**: 有股怪味
  - *Example*: Okay, um, don't take this the wrong way, but your place kinda has a weird smell.
- **It's two gifts in one**: 一份礼物两用的/二合一
  - *Example*: It's two gifts in one. It's a pen that's also a clock! Huh?
- **It's not something a boyfriend gives**: 这不是男朋友该送的东西（暗示礼物太随意/不走心）
  - *Example*: While this is a very nice gift, maybe it's just not something a boyfriend gives?
- **a book that's also a safe**: 一个同时是保险箱的书（Joey 的逻辑：多功能才值）
  - *Example*: Is it like a book that's also a safe?
- **the end of time, and garbage is all that has survived**: 世界末日，垃圾是唯一存活下来的东西（Ross 的夸张比喻）
  - *Example*: Well, like that, only instead of a chair, it's a pile of garbage. And instead of the end of the day, it's the end of time, and garbage is all that has survived!
- **It's endearing, really**: 其实挺可爱的/令人怜爱的（Ross 自我催眠，试图接受脏乱）
  - *Example*: It's, uh... it's endearing, really.
- **Department of Sanitation**: 环卫局（Ross 被 Cheryl 蒙眼猜人时的搞笑回答，暗示她公寓脏得像垃圾场）
  - *Example*: (sneaking up behind Ross) Guess who? / Department of Sanitation?
- **part time caterers, and we have no place else to go**: 兼职餐饮承办人，而且我们没别的地方可去（Phoebe 对寡妇的真诚宣言）
  - *Example*: We're part time caterers, and we have no place else to go.

## 5. 实用短句

- **I'd rather not**: 我不太想（委婉拒绝）
  - *Example*: I'd rather not.
- **Don't take this the wrong way**: 别误会/别往心里去（铺垫不太好说的话）
  - *Example*: Okay, um, don't take this the wrong way, but your place kinda has a weird smell.
- **while you're at it**: 既然你要做那个，顺便……
  - *Example*: Thanks, man. And oh, while you're at it, could you get her a card?
- **Would you like me to...?**: 要不要我帮你……？（略带讽刺的主动提议）
  - *Example*: Would you like me to write her a little poem as well?
- **Can you give me a hand with...?**: 能帮我一把吗？/搭把手？
  - *Example*: Can you give me a hand with all this stuff?
- **It came out to an even twenty**: 刚好二十块整（even = 整数的）
  - *Example*: No, it came out to an even twenty.
- **That's almost as much as a new book**: 那差不多是一本新书的价格了（Joey 嫌贵）
  - *Example*: Wow. That's almost as much as a new book.
- **I owe you one**: 我欠你一个人情
  - *Example*: Thanks man. Thanks for doing this, I owe you one.
- **We provided a service, and we deserve to be paid**: 我们提供了服务，理应得到报酬
  - *Example*: We provided a service, and we deserve to be paid because you ate that service.
- **You really didn't have to**: 你真的不用这么客气（收到礼物时的礼貌用语）
  - *Example*: You really didn't have to. (Opens the box) Wow.
- **I kinda have the feeling you had something to do with it**: 我有种感觉这事跟你有关系
  - *Example*: I kinda have the feeling you had something to do with it.
- **By the way, in case you missed that, that sound was, "Uhl, ell."**: 顺便说一下，万一你没听到，刚才那个声音是"嗯……呃"（Chandler 紧张到自我解嘲）
  - *Example*: By the way, in case you missed that, that sound was, "Uhl, ell."

## 6. 剧情词汇

- **Cheryl**: Ross 约会的漂亮金发女生，古生物学博士候选人，但公寓脏得像垃圾场
- **Mitzi**: Cheryl 的仓鼠，Ross 第二次上门时把薯片袋里动的东西当成 Mitzi 用球拍猛打，结果只是一只老鼠
- ***The Velveteen Rabbit***: 经典儿童绘本《绒布小兔子》，Chandler 费尽心思找到早期版本作为 Kathy 的生日礼物
- **Cinnamon Fruit Toasties**: 肉桂水果吐司饼干，Ross 试图用来引诱 Cheryl 去他家
- **homo-erectus**: 直立人（古人类学术语），Ross 用来回答 Cheryl 的邀约，暗示"当然要去"
- **centazoic era**: 新生代（古生物地质年代），Cheryl 的研究方向，Chandler 吐槽是"最简单的时代"
- ***Rent***: 音乐剧《吉屋出租》，1996 年托尼奖最佳音乐剧，Rachel 用填字游戏暗示 Chandler 答出答案
- ***Grease***: 音乐剧《油脂》，Chandler 猜错的答案
- **Office Max**: 美国办公用品连锁超市，Chandler 讽刺 Joey 买礼物像买文具
- **Hanukkah**: 犹太光明节，持续八晚每晚送小礼物，Chandler 用来讽刺那支笔-clock 太廉价
- **Angela Delveccio**: Joey 的前女友，交往三年竟然没赶上过生日（Chandler 用来证明 Joey 不会送礼物）
- **Mrs. Burkart**: 丧偶的遗孀，丈夫 Jack 生前管财务，Monica 不好意思催账，Phoebe 出马搞定
- **Gunther**: Central Perk 咖啡店的暗恋 Rachel 的店员，听到 Rachel 独立完成填字游戏后冲出来想拥抱她结果滑倒
- **Kathy**: Joey 的女朋友，Chandler 暗恋的对象，收到绒布兔子后猜到是 Chandler 帮忙找的
- **Department of Sanitation**: 纽约环卫局，Ross 被 Cheryl 蒙眼时以为对方是环卫工人（暗示公寓太脏）
- **bologna**: 意大利博洛尼亚香肠片，Ross 亲热时手摸到的不明物体
