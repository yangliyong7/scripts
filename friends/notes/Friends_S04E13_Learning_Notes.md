## 5. 实用短句

- **I made a pile of your stuff**: 我把你的东西堆成了一堆。
  - *Example*: Hey, Rach! I made a pile of your stuff over on this side of the room.
- **What can I do for you?**: 我能为你做什么？
  - *Example*: Hi, I'm Rachel Green. What can I do for you Joshua?
- **I'm so sorry**: 我很抱歉。
  - *Example*: Oh, I'm so sorry.
- **I got away with two things**: 我只保住了两样东西。
  - *Example*: I got away with two things. This suit and what turned out to be a skirt.
- **It wasn't much fun**: 不怎么好玩。
  - *Example*: Yeah, but it wasn't much fun dropping it off at the dry cleaners in the skirt.
- **I'm all yours**: 我全听你的/我是你的了。
  - *Example*: So I need everything down to underwear, so if you're willing, I'm all yours.
- **I really need to talk to you**: 我真的需要跟你谈谈。
  - *Example*: Mr. Waltham, I ah really need to talk to you.
- **In a moment, please**: 请稍等一下。
  - *Example*: In a moment, please, I'm in the middle of a task.
- **let me just get this straight**: 让我把话说清楚。
  - *Example*: Okay, so let me just get this straight. You're accusing me of cheating on you, and insulting my performance?
- **throwing that in my face**: 拿那件事来刺激我/翻旧账。
  - *Example*: I can't believe you're throwing that in my face.
- **don't expect that to happen anytime soon**: 别指望那很快会发生。
  - *Example*: Yeah, well, don't expect that to happen anytime soon!
- **I happen to have tickets too**: 我刚好也有票。
  - *Example*: You know, its funny, basketball, because I happen to have tickets too…
- **I got the date, I'm just not on it**: 约会是约成了，只是赴约的人不是我。
  - *Example*: He didn't turn me down! He's at the game isn't he? I got the date, I'm just not on it!
