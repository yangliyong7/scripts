# Friends S04E12 口语表达笔记

## 1. 情绪与反应

- **What the hell is that?!!**: 那到底是什么鬼东西？！（Rachel 被公鸡打鸣吵醒）
  - *Example*: What the hell is that?!! (to Monica) What the hell is that? Is that you?
- **Boy, you are really not a morning person**: 天哪，你真不是个能早起的人。
  - *Example*: Boy, you are really not a morning person.
- **BACK OFF!!!**: 给我让开！！！（Rachel 暴怒砸门）
  - *Example*: (angrily) BACK OFF!!! (She starts banging on their door.)
- **I'm so not impressed**: 我一点也不佩服。
  - *Example*: I'm so not impressed. Everybody snacks when they shop.
- **That is sooo not true!**: 才不是呢！（强烈否认）
  - *Example*: That is sooo not true!
- **Aww, man!**: 哎，真倒霉！（输了比赛时的感叹）
  - *Example*: Aww, man!
- **Wow! You guys really don't know anything!**: 哇！你们真的什么都不知道！
  - *Example*: Wow! You guys really don't know anything!
- **NOOOOOOOOO!!!!!**: 不！！！！（Monica 输掉比赛的绝望嘶吼）
  - *Example*: NOOOOOOOOO!!!!!

## 2. 决策与行动

- **going through some changes**: 正在经历一些变化（Joey 委婉地说小鸡在变）
  - *Example*: It's the chick! She's…going through some changes.
- **We're getting a second opinion**: 我们准备再看一个医生/听听别的意见。
  - *Example*: Well the vet seems to think that's she's becoming a rooster. We're getting a second opinion.
- **think thick**: 想着变厚（Phoebe 让大家帮她"意念增厚"子宫内膜）
  - *Example*: Okay everyone, think thick.
- **I'm gonna go to the fertility doctor**: 我要去看生育科医生。
  - *Example*: All right. I'm gonna go to the fertility doctor and um, see if I'm ready to have Frank and Alice's embryo transferred into my uterus.
- **Challenge extended / Challenge excepted**: 挑战升级了 / 接受挑战。
  - *Example*: Challenge extended. / Challenge excepted.
- **Ten bucks says that we can name every item in that bag**: 我赌十块钱我们能说出袋子里每一样东西。
  - *Example*: Okay, ten bucks says that we can name every item in that bag.
- **Fork it over! Cough it up! Pay the piper! Gimme it**: 交出来！掏钱！付账！给我！（各种"给钱"的说法）
  - *Example*: All right! Ten buck! Fork it over! Cough it up! Pay the piper! Gimme it.
- **I want a rematch**: 我要再赛一场。
  - *Example*: That does not mean you know us better, I-I want a rematch.
- **raise the stakes**: 提高赌注。
  - *Example*: Hey, don't get mad at us! No one forced you to raise the stakes!
- **I call Monica's room!**: 我要 Monica 的房间！（先声明占有）
  - *Example*: I call Monica's room!
- **standard shotgun rules**: 标准的"先看到先占有"规则。
  - *Example*: Sure I can, standard shotgun rules, I'm sight of the room and I called it.
- **We're just gonna lie here, let gravity do its job**: 我们就躺这儿，让重力发挥作用。
  - *Example*: I'm just gonna y'know, lie it your chair, Y'know? Yeah, good, I'm let gravity y'know, do its jobs.

## 3. 社交与关系

- **dumb-ass rooster**: 蠢公鸡（骂人的说法）
  - *Example*: Well, I've been up since six. Thanks to somebody's dumb-ass rooster.
- **old lady underpants**: 老太太内裤（Joey 调侃 Monica 穿的土气内裤）
  - *Example*: She's mad because I know today's her laundry day and that means she's wearing her old lady underpants.
- **You can only eat Tic Tacks in even numbers**: 你只吃偶数颗薄荷糖（Chandler 对 Monica 的了解）
  - *Example*: You can only eat Tic Tacks in even numbers.
- **a half-eaten box of cookies**: 一盒吃了一半的饼干。
  - *Example*: Ross, I believe, if you check Rachel's bag you will find a half-eaten box of cookies in there.
- **she's a woman, not a gumball machine**: 她是女人，不是糖果贩卖机（Alice 反驳 Frank 说塞 200 个胚胎）
  - *Example*: Sweety, now, she's a woman, not a gumball machine.
- **putting all of their eggs in my basket**: 把所有鸡蛋都放在我的篮子里（把全部希望寄托在我身上）
  - *Example*: They are literally putting all of their eggs in my basket.
- **Hey, no way, that rooster's family!**: 不行，那只公鸡是家人！
  - *Example*: Hey, no way, that rooster's family!
- **You can't just ignore the bet! It's a bet!**: 你不能无视赌约！赌约就是赌约！
  - *Example*: You can't just ignore the bet! It's a bet! You bet and you bet and if you lose, you lose the bet!
- **girl roommate**: 女室友。
  - *Example*: Cool, girl roommate.

## 4. 描述事物

- **0-Dark:30**: 凌晨超级早的时候（幽默说法，意为"黑得伸手不见五指的 0 点 30"）
  - *Example*: it's 0-Dark:30, in other words it's really, really early.
- **not a morning person**: 不是早起型的人。
  - *Example*: Boy, you are really not a morning person.
- **endometria layer is thick**: 子宫内膜层厚（医学术语）
  - *Example*: They're just gonna umm, look to see if my endometria layer is thick.
- **a 25% chance that at least one will attach**: 至少一个能着床的概率是 25%。
  - *Example*: We do five because that gives you a 25% chance that at least one will attach.
- **it costs $16,000 each time you do this**: 每次做这个要 16000 美元。
  - *Example*: Umm, it costs $16,000 each time you do this.
- **That's a lot of pressure on me and my uterus**: 这对我跟我的子宫压力好大。
  - *Example*: Whoa!! That—okay, that's a lot of pressure on me and my uterus.
- **coming down with something**: 感觉要生病了。
  - *Example*: Man, I feel like I'm coming down with something.
- **Steady as a rock!**: 手稳如磐石！（Monica 证明自己很冷静）
  - *Example*: No! Steady as a rock! Now, are you with me.
- **transpondster**: 编造的词（Rachel 瞎猜 Chandler 的职业）
  - *Example*: Oh-oh-oh, he's a transponce—transpondster!
- **That's not even a word!**: 那根本不是一个词！
  - *Example*: That's not even a word! I can get this! I can get this!

  - *Example*: Nope, not knocked up yet.
- **maybe if we keep that drawer shut, it'll die**: 也许我们把抽屉关紧，里面的东西就会死掉。
  - *Example*: I don't know! But maybe if we keep that drawer shut, it'll die.

## 6. 剧情词汇

- **The One With The Embryos**: 本集标题，指 Phoebe 接受胚胎植入
- **rooster**: 公鸡，Joey 和 Chandler 的小鸡原来不是母鸡而是公鸡
- **chick / duck**: 小鸡和小鸭，Joey 和 Chandler 的宠物
- **embryo**: 胚胎，Frank 和 Alice 的试管婴儿胚胎
- **uterus**: 子宫，Phoebe 代孕用的
- **endometria layer**: 子宫内膜层，着床需要够厚
- **implantation**: 胚胎着床/植入
- **fertility doctor / Dr. Zane**: 生育科医生
- **Frank and Alice**: Phoebe 的弟弟和弟媳，不能自然怀孕
- **pet peeve**: 最讨厌的事（Ross 出的问答题类别）
- **Fears and Pet Peeves / Ancient History / Literature / It's All Relative**: Ross 设计的问答题四大类别
- **Lightning Round**: 闪电问答环节（加时赛）
- **foosball table**: 桌上足球台，Chandler 和 Joey 搬进新公寓后搬入
- **Miss Chinandolor Bong**: Chandler 和 Joey 订 TV Guide 时用的假名
- **Viva Las Gaygas**: Chandler 爸爸在拉斯维加斯的变装秀
- **Big fat goalie**: Monica 当曲棍球守门员时的绰号
- **Dangerous Liaisons / Weekend at Bernie's**: Rachel 声称喜欢的电影和实际喜欢的电影
- **Maurice**: Joey 小时候想象中的朋友，职业是 space cowboy
- **Althea**: Monica 和 Ross 已故祖母的名字
- **Tic Tacks**: 一种薄荷糖，Monica 只吃偶数颗
- **the big fake dog / ceramic dog**: 那只大陶瓷狗，两队搬公寓时作为战利品
- **Adidas**: Phoebe 给胚胎唱的歌里提到要给他们买阿迪达斯
