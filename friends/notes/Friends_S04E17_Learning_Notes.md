# Friends S04E17 口语表达笔记

## 1. 情绪与反应
- **Whoa!**: 哇！/ 慢着！（惊讶）。
  - *Example*: **Whoa**! Is this porn?
- **all kinds of naked**: 各种意义上都赤裸裸 / 脱得一丝不挂（夸张）。
  - *Example*: That lady's **all kinds of naked**.
- **And I'm sad**: 于是我很难过（Treeger 关掉免费频道后的丧气收尾）。
  - *Example*: I never got it back again. **And I'm sad**.
- **all freaked**: 完全慌了 / 吓坏了。
  - *Example*: I was so afraid you were going to be **all freaked**.
- **Back to happy!**: 快回到开心模式！（Phoebe 发现自己说多了赶紧圆场）。
  - *Example*: No, **back to happy**. **Back to happy**!
- **It was dreadful**: 糟透了（英式；Emily 形容航班和自己的反应）。
  - *Example*: **It was dreadful**.
- **I'm the stupid moron who…**: 我就是那个傻到……的笨蛋（Ross 自嘲蹲了一夜）。
  - *Example*: **I'm the stupid moron who** spent the whole night outside her apartment!

## 2. 决策与行动
- **come out and say it**: 直接说出口 / 不再憋着。
  - *Example*: I'm just gonna **come out and say it**. Okay? I think I love you.
- **play hard to get**: 欲擒故纵（Joey 乱给建议）。
  - *Example*: You **play hard to get**.
- **forget it, it's over**: 算了，完了（Chandler 断言表白失败）。
  - *Example*: You told her you love her, **it's over**.
- **talk me into**: 说服某人去做某事。
  - *Example*: If I hadn't let you **talk me into** going to the airport…
- **prove your love**: 证明你的爱（Monica 催 Ross 去伦敦）。
  - *Example*: Now you have to **prove your love**!
- **fight for her**: 为她争取 / 去挽回她。
  - *Example*: You have to go **fight for her**!
- **Don't let her go without a fight**: 别不争不抢就放她走。
  - *Example*: **Don't let her go without a fight**!
- **drop out of college**: 辍学。
  - *Example*: The best thing for me to do is to **drop out of college** and get a job.
- **give up on your dream**: 放弃梦想。
  - *Example*: You can't **give up on your dream**.
- **turn it off**: 关掉（电视）；本集男生死也不敢关。
  - *Example*: Why don't you just **turn it off**?

## 3. 社交与关系
- **said our good-byes**: 已经道别过了。
  - *Example*: We-we **said our good-byes** this morning.
- **fall madly in love**: 疯狂坠入爱河（Monica 的幻想）。
  - *Example*: Meet someone from a strange land, **fall in madly love**…
- **there's someone else**: 另有其人 / 我有别人了。
  - *Example*: There's something that I've got to tell you, **there's someone else**.
- **leave certain things to the imagination**: 有些事留给想象（Rachel 说 Joshua 不看黄片）。
  - *Example*: He prefers to **leave certain things to the imagination**.
- **it's all over with Colin**: 和 Colin 彻底结束了。
  - *Example*: It's **all over with Colin**.
- **I love you / Thank you**: 表白 vs 「谢谢」——尴尬不对等回复。
  - *Example*: I said, "I love you." And she said, "**Thank you**."
- **Way to be strong, man!**: 好样的，够坚强！（表面夸，背后做 loser 手势）。
  - *Example*: **Way to be strong, man!**

## 4. 描述事物
- **snaking the shower drain**: 用管道疏通器通淋浴下水道。
  - *Example*: Treeger's **snaking the shower drain**.
- **protective porn bubble**: 「成人频道防护泡」——谁也别碰电视周围。
  - *Example*: Imagine a **protective porn bubble** if you will.
- **blocking the porn**: 挡着黄片画面。
  - *Example*: Pheebs! You're **blocking the porn**!
- **multiple births / triplets**: 多胞胎 / 三胞胎。
  - *Example*: Have we talked about the possibility of **multiple births**?
- **log rides**: 乐园里的激流木船项目（Phoebe 形容三胞胎「冲出来」）。
  - *Example*: It's gonna be one of those **log rides** where they just come shooting out!
- **super fertile**: 超能生 / 超级容易怀孕。
  - *Example*: His **super fertile** sister is having three babies!
- **sweatshop**: 血汗工厂（Alice 开玩笑说让学生做婴儿服）。
  - *Example*: It'll be like my very own little **sweatshop**.
- **corny**: 老土、尴尬的（对白）。
  - *Example*: Some of the dialogue was **corny**.

## 5. 实用短句
- **What in the name of hell?**: 这到底是什么鬼？！（加强版 What the hell）
  - *Example*: **What in the name of hell?**
- **It was like finding money**: 就像捡到钱一样（Treeger 形容撞上免费频道）。
  - *Example*: Bam! **It was like finding money**.
- **We don't know what could make this go away**: 我们不知道怎样会让它消失（所以谁也不敢动）。
  - *Example*: **We don't know what could make this go away**.
- **cheer up**: 振作一点。
  - *Example*: Hey, **cheer up**! You're gonna see her again, right?
- **I've been watching too much porn**: 我黄片看太多了（Monica 幻想跑偏后自嘲）。
  - *Example*: **I've been watching too much porn**.
- **Doctors are wrong all the time**: 医生经常搞错（医生安慰 Phoebe）。
  - *Example*: **Doctors are wrong all the time**.
- **Does that mean the same thing in England…**: 在英国是不是也这个意思？（Ross 不敢相信「有别人」）。
  - *Example*: **Does that mean the same thing in England as it does in America?**
- **That'll scare her!**: 那只会吓跑她！（Joey 反讽去伦敦的主意）。
  - *Example*: So yeah, go to London **that'll scare her**!
- **bugger off**: 滚开（英式脏话；Emily 对偷听邻居说）。
  - *Example*: Mrs. Newman if you're listening, **bugger off**.
- **FREE PORN!!!**: 免费黄片还在！！！（结尾重开电视的欢呼）。
  - *Example*: **FREE PORN!!!**
