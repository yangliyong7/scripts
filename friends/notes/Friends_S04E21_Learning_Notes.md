# Friends S04E21 口语表达笔记

## 1. 情绪与反应
- **Everybody's doing stuff!**: 大家都在搞大事！（Joey 感慨身边人都在结婚、怀孕）。
  - *Example*: **Everybody's doing stuff**!
- **WE WERE ON A BREAK!!!!!!!**: 我们当时分手了！！！（Ross 闪回里爆发，全剧梗）。
  - *Example*: **WE WERE ON A BREAK!!!!!!!**
- **kick-you-in-the-crotch, spit-on-your-neck fantastic**: 好到踢你裆、吐你脖子→Rachel 讽刺 Ross 带 Julie 回来。
  - *Example*: Isn't that just **kick-you-in-the-crotch, spit-on-your-neck fantastic**?
- **Ohhhhhhhh God**: 天哪……（Rachel 留言被 Ross 听到后社死）。
  - *Example*: **Ohhhhhhhh God**. (climbs off his back)
- **I'll work on it**: 我会努力的→嘴上说为 Ross 开心，其实还得消化。
  - *Example*: I really—I'm-I'm happ—**I'll work on it**.

## 2. 决策与行动
- **we have to do something huge**: 我们得干件大事（Chandler 不想人生虚度）。
  - *Example*: Look, **we have to do something**. Okay? **Something huge**!
- **not something stupid**: 不是蠢事，是大事（Chandler 否决爬珠峰）。
  - *Example*: No-no, **not something stupid**, something huge.
- **get off our buts**: 从屁股上爬起来行动起来（口语拼写 butt）。
  - *Example*: One of these days we're **get off of our buts** and rent Die Hard again!
- **invite whoever you want**: 想请谁请谁（Ross 对 Emily 退让，闪回起点）。
  - *Example*: Yeah, fine, **invite whoever you want**.
- **makes up his mind and mails it**: 下定决心寄出（Ross 寄 Rachel 请柬）。
  - *Example*: Finally, he **makes up his mind and mails it**.
- **think about it**: 考虑一下（Monica 劝 Rachel 是否去婚礼）。
  - *Example*: Just say you'll **think about it**, okay?

## 3. 社交与关系
- **Phoebe is, making people**: 菲比在造人（Chandler 拐弯说 Phoebe 代孕）。
  - *Example*: **Phoebe is, making people**.
- **old lovers**: 旧情人（Emily 暗示不想请 Rachel）。
  - *Example*: I don't think I'd be comfortable with any of my **old lovers** there.
- **absolutely your decision**: 完全由你决定→表面客气，实则施压（Emily）。
  - *Example*: But **it's absolutely your decision**. More tea?
- **two-week thing, no commitment**: 两周玩玩、不承诺（Ross 谈 Emily）。
  - *Example*: We both agreed that it was gonna be a **two-week thing**, y'know **no commitment**.
- **When were you under me?**: 你什么时候在我下面？→听错/故意曲解 *over me*（还爱着我吗）。
  - *Example*: When, when were you... **under me**?
- **It's always been you**: 一直是你（Ross 选 Rachel 经典句）。
  - *Example*: **It's always been you**, Rach.
- **he's her lobster**: 他是她的龙虾→Phoebe 的「灵魂伴侣」理论。
  - *Example*: See, **he's her lobster**.

## 4. 描述事物
- **ass print on this chair**: 椅子上的屁股印→Chandler 自嘲在咖啡馆虚度人生。
  - *Example*: The only way people would even know I was here, would be by the **ass print on this chair**!
- **ship has sailed**: 机会已错过/船已开走了（Ross 说和 Julie 在一起）。
  - *Example*: I'm happy. **This ship has sailed**.
- **I don't need your stupid ship**: 我才不要你那破船→Rachel 回怼双关。
  - *Example*: 'Cause **I don't need your stupid ship**.
- **a break from us**: 分开一段时间（不是冷静吃冻酸奶，是分手）。
  - *Example*: No. A **break from us**.
- **Nobody likes change**: 没人喜欢改变（Ross 被问 Chloe 好不好时的敷衍）。
  - *Example*: **Nobody likes change**.
- **third trimester**: 孕晚期（Phoebe 不能飞伦敦）。
  - *Example*: You can't fly in your **third trimester**!

## 5. 实用短句
- **in a million years**: 一百万年也不会→强调绝不可能（便宜 uncle 不会飞伦敦）。
  - *Example*: He'd never fly to London **in a million years**.
- **Handkerchief? No-no, I got my invitation**: 手帕？不，我有请柬→Joey 说请柬里夹纸巾可以当手帕哭婚礼。
  - *Example*: "Handkerchief?" "No-no, **I got my invitation**."
- **If she doesn't come, we don't come**: 她不去我们也不去？（Chandler 怕 Rachel 不去自己白买票）。
  - *Example*: This isn't one of those **"If she doesn't come, we-we don't come,"** right?
- **for the best**: 这样最好（Rachel 决定不去婚礼自我说服）。
  - *Example*: It's just, **it's for the best**, y'know it is.
- **go into labour**: 要生了/临盆（Rachel 借口留下来陪 Phoebe）。
  - *Example*: Drive her to the hospital in case she **goes into labour**.
- **almost as good as being there**: 几乎跟亲临一样（伦敦博物馆录像书）。
  - *Example*: **It's almost as good as being there**.
- **You can't go to a museum in your underwear**: 你不能穿内裤逛博物馆（Joey 觉得录像更好）。
  - *Example*: **You can't go to a museum in your underwear**!
- **probably just the one time**: 大概只能一次（Chandler 吐槽穿内裤去博物馆会被赶）。
  - *Example*: Well, You could, but... **probably just the one time**.
- **general wondering... ment**: 不算是问题，更像是在想……（Ross 紧张搭讪结巴造词）。
  - *Example*: It's not so much a question as.. more of a **general wondering... ment**.

## 6. 剧情词汇
- **The Invitation**: 本集核心道具；Ross 是否寄给 Rachel
- **Clip show framing**: 闪回剪辑集；Ross/Rachel 双视角
- **Emily / London wedding**: Ross 未婚妻；婚礼在伦敦
- **Ben addressed them**: 请柬上字迹说是 Ben 写的（Emily 圆场）
- **Prom Video**: 毕业舞会录像；「lobster」出处
- **Mark / Chloe / Julie**: 闪回中的感情线人物
- **Die Hard**: Chandler & Joey 伦敦计划梗（与 E23 呼应）
- **Mt. Everest video**: 开场妄想爬珠峰改租录像
