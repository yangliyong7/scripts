# Friends S04E05 口语表达笔记

## 1. 情绪与反应

- **What's the worst thing that could happen?**: 最坏能怎样？（Monica 鼓励 Chandler 去搭讪）
  - *Example*: Just go up to her and ask her out. Oh, what's the worst thing that could happen?
- **I could die**: 我可能会死（Chandler 的回答，夸张表达搭讪的恐惧）
  - *Example*: I could die.
- **Wish me luck**: 祝我好运吧（出发做事前的口语）
  - *Example*: (softly) Wish me luck.
- **give me a break**: 饶了我吧/别开玩笑了（表达无奈或被调侃后的反应）
  - *Example*: Hey, come on, give me a break, I'm out on a limb here.
- **lay off him**: 放过他/别逼他了
  - *Example*: Come on, Pheebs lay off him.
- **I can't get her out of my head**: 我怎么也忘不了她/脑子里全是她
  - *Example*: I can't--I just, I can't get her out of my head. Y'know?
- **You mean you stole it!**: 你是说你偷了我的！（Phoebe 认为感冒是她的财产，Monica 传染等于偷）
  - *Example*: You mean you stole it! Don't cover your mouth when you do that!
- **Ohhhh!! Gross!!**: 好恶心！！（Monica 看到 Phoebe 舔碗边时的反应）
  - *Example*: Ohhhh!! Gross!!
- **I'm in love with Joey's girlfriend**: 我爱上 Joey 的女朋友了（Chandler 的坦白）
  - *Example*: Okay. You were right. I'm in love with Joey's girlfriend.

## 2. 决策与行动

- **ask her out**: 约她出去（ask out = 邀请约会）
  - *Example*: Just go up to her and ask her out.
- **get shot down**: 被拒绝（shot down 原意"被击落"，口语中指搭讪/请求被拒）
  - *Example*: All right, I'm gonna do it! I'm gonna get shot down. Any advice?
- **figure out a way to**: 想办法去……
  - *Example*: I was just trying to figure out a way to demonstrate how I could get my exceptionally large feet into my even bigger mouth.
- **break open a bottle of wine**: 开一瓶酒（break open = 打开，带庆祝/放松的语气）
  - *Example*: I'm thinking Amanda and I break open a bottle of wine, and do a little "playing" ourselves.
- **be a good little boy and...**: 乖乖去帮我……（带哄小孩的语气使唤别人）
  - *Example*: Hey Gunther, be a good little boy and bring me a whiskey.
- **make an effort**: 努力一把/下点功夫
  - *Example*: Look, I made an effort to like Janice, now I think it's your turn to make an effort to like Kathy.
- **grab a beer**: 去拿杯啤酒（grab = 随便拿/取，口语中 grab 很常用）
  - *Example*: Listen, I-I'm gonna grab a beer.
- **tone it down a little**: 稍微收敛一点/低调一点
  - *Example*: Well, that's pretty good. But you might wanna tone it down a little.
- **head out / head**: 走人/出发（head 作动词 = 朝某个方向走）
  - *Example*: Well, it's getting late, I've got to get to the game, so I'm gonna... head.

## 3. 社交与关系

- **out on a limb**: 冒险/处于不利位置（字面：爬到树枝末端，比喻冒险）
  - *Example*: Hey, come on, give me a break, I'm out on a limb here.
- **my exceptionally large feet into my even bigger mouth**: 把我的大脚塞进更大的嘴里（英语习语 put your foot in your mouth = 说错话、出糗，Chandler 把习语扩展成夸张自嘲）
  - *Example*: I was just trying to figure out a way to demonstrate how I could get my exceptionally large feet into my even bigger mouth.
- **You barely know her!**: 你跟她几乎不熟！
  - *Example*: Well, how-how-how is that possible? You barely know her!
- **I thought we had a deal**: 我以为我们说好了/有约定
  - *Example*: Kathy was being really nice and you just walked away. I thought we had a deal.
- **if that's too damned difficult for you, then the least you can do is pretend**: 如果那对你太难了，至少你可以装一下
  - *Example*: I want you to like her! But if that's too damned difficult for you, then the least you can do is pretend.
- **double date**: 双人约会（两对情侣一起出去）
  - *Example*: Y'know, I have a friend you would like, she's really pretty. And then we could double date!
- **not really my type**: 不太是我喜欢的类型（委婉表达不感兴趣）
  - *Example*: Ohh, she's-she's not really my type.
- **It's so nice to finally be in a fun relationship**: 终于能谈一段有趣的恋爱真好
  - *Example*: It's so nice to finally be in a fun relationship, y'know? There's nothing boring about him.
- **You have a 12-year-old girl's job!**: 你干的活是12岁小女孩干的！（Monica 戳穿 Ross 其实只是在当保姆）
  - *Example*: You're not even going out! You're her baby sitter! You have a 12-year-old girl's job!
- **are we cool?**: 我们没事了吧？/我们和好了吗？（cool = 没问题/关系正常）
  - *Example*: So umm, are we cool?

## 4. 描述事物

- **I'm unemployed, my music is all I really have now**: 我失业了，音乐是我现在唯一的寄托
  - *Example*: But I'm unemployed, my music is all I really have now.
- **I love how sexy I am**: 我爱死了自己性感的样子（Phoebe 感冒后嗓音变沙哑反而觉得好听）
  - *Example*: It's fun, God I love how sexy I am.
- **nothing that sad has ever really happened to me**: 我身上从没发生过什么真正悲伤的事（Phoebe 的自嘲式天真）
  - *Example*: I need to write some depressing stuff to go along with my new floozy voice, but nothing that sad has ever really happened to me.
- **I don't mean to be a square**: 我不想太古板（square = 古板的人/守旧的人）
  - *Example*: Oh, I don't mean to be a square, but I'd really appreciate it if you wait and drink your wine after the kids are asleep?
- **she's gorgeous**: 她太漂亮了
  - *Example*: Not your type?! She's gorgeous!
- **wanna-slit-my-own-throat in love**: 爱到想割自己喉咙的程度（Chandler 用极端夸张形容自己的暗恋之苦）
  - *Example*: That I'm so fantastically, over-the-top, wanna-slit-my-own-throat in love with her.
- **over-the-top**: 过分的/极端的（口语常用，形容某事超出正常范围）
  - *Example*: That I'm so fantastically, over-the-top, wanna-slit-my-own-throat in love with her.
- **Beer's beer man, 24, 7!!**: 啤酒就是啤酒，24小时7天随时喝！（Josh 的青春热血口号）
  - *Example*: So I'll see you at the party? Beer's beer man, 24, 7!!

## 5. 实用短句

- **Just be yourself. But, not too much.**: 做你自己就好，但别太过了。（Monica 给 Chandler 搭讪建议的经典毒舌）
  - *Example*: Just be yourself. But, not too much.
- **I tell people secrets. It makes them like me.**: 我告诉别人秘密，这会让他们喜欢我。（Chandler 的自嘲式解释）
  - *Example*: I tell people secrets. It makes them like me.
- **Didn't I tell ya?**: 我不是跟你说了吗？
  - *Example*: Didn't I tell ya? Always showin' off.
- **Way to go!**: 干得好！（鼓励/赞扬的口语）
  - *Example*: Way to go, Phoebe!
- **I could write about the time...**: 我可以写那次……（引出回忆）
  - *Example*: Oh, yeah, I could write about the time my hair did that "Woo-hoo" thing.
- **It's my pleasure**: 我的荣幸/不用客气
  - *Example*: Oh no-no-no, it's my pleasure.
- **help yourself to anything**: 随便用/随便吃（招待客人时的常用语）
  - *Example*: My cell phone number is right here on the counter, please help yourself to anything in the fridge.
- **Understanding a little more why you're single**: 越来越理解你为什么单身了（Kathy 被 Chandler 的 fishnet stockings 梗无语后的吐槽）
  - *Example*: Okay. Understanding a little more why you're single.
- **what do you want from me?**: 你想让我怎样？
  - *Example*: Hey, look, what do you want from me?
- **My treat**: 我请客
  - *Example*: Good, and hey! My treat.
- **At least I made ten bucks in my relationship**: 至少我在感情里还赚了十块钱（Ross 自嘲 Amanda 的约会对象给了他小费）
  - *Example*: At least I made ten bucks in my relationship.
- **Would you both please start acting like adults?**: 你们俩能不能像个大人？
  - *Example*: Now, would you both please start acting like adults? And get me my cough drops!

## 6. 剧情词汇

- **Kathy**: Joey 在表演课上认识的女朋友，Chandler 一见钟情却只能暗恋的对象，本集核心三角恋开端
- **Amanda**: Ross 约会一次的单亲妈妈，Ross 以为在约会实际是被请去当保姆看孩子
- **Josh**: Rachel 的新男友，年轻的大学生运动员，Rachel 怀疑他偷自己的东西
- **Gunther**: Central Perk 咖啡店员工，暗恋 Rachel，被 Phoebe 强吻后居然跑来跟 Rachel 道歉
- **Chucky Cheese**: 美国连锁儿童娱乐餐厅，有电子动画表演，Rachel 用来嘲笑 Ross 的"约会"只是带孩子玩
- **Ernie Kovacs**: 美国早期电视喜剧先驱（剧中拼作 Ernie Cofax），Chandler 和 Kathy 深夜一起看的节目
- ***The Mod Squad***: 1968-73 年美国警匪电视剧，Chandler 追 Kathy 时的背景音乐
- **Wonder Broom**: 神奇扫帚，Chandler 和 Joey 家的一件道具，Chandler 和 Kathy 用它划船玩耍
- **Commodore 64**: 1982 年推出的经典家用电脑，Rachel 用来嘲讽 Ross 高中时只会在电脑上排赛程
- **Smelly Cat**: Phoebe 的标志性原创歌曲
- **Pepper People**: Phoebe 的另一首原创歌曲，她说最后一节的喷嚏是故意的
- **Hide-and-Go-Seek**: 捉迷藏游戏，Chandler 和小鸡小鸭玩但动物完全不动
- **fishnet stockings**: 渔网袜，Chandler 用来推脱不想看脱衣舞女的借口
- **cough drops**: 润喉糖/止咳含片，Monica 感冒后一直让 Ross 和 Rachel 帮她拿
- **paper mache man**: 纸糊人，Phoebe 在 Closing Credits 中唱的歌曲主题
- **Platting goats**: 编织山羊，Phoebe 感冒好了之后唱的新歌，Gunther 说喜欢（可能是讽刺）
