# Friends S04E07 口语表达笔记

## 1. 情绪与反应

- **call me crazy**: 说我疯了吧（引出大胆猜测时的自嘲开场白）
  - *Example*: And call me crazy, but I think she likes me too.
- **you're in for a big surprise**: 你等着大吃一惊吧 / 你可要大失所望了（in for = 注定要经历，多含负面预期）
  - *Example*: Y'know if we ever go to war and you're captured, you're in for a **big** surprise.
- **keeps getting worse and worse**: 越来越糟 / 雪上加霜
  - *Example*: It just keeps getting worse and worse!
- **It was like torture!**: 那简直像受罪！（夸张表达煎熬感）
  - *Example*: I just walked in the bathroom and saw Kathy naked! It was like torture!
- **At a loss for words**: 无言以对 / 不知说什么好（书面固定搭配）
  - *Example*: (At a loss for words) Boy, that was-that was, umm… terrific.
- **he's lost it**: 他疯了 / 他失去理智了（俚语）
  - *Example*: Oh my God, he's lost it. He's totally lost it.
- **Sound good?**: 这样行吗？ / 怎么样？（提建议后的征询）
  - *Example*: Even if it means we're unhappy forever. Sound good?
- **I feel horrible**: 我觉得很愧疚 / 心里很过意不去
  - *Example*: Yes. Yes! Right! And I feel horrible. You have to believe me!

## 2. 决策与行动

- **it's time for you to settle down**: 你该安定下来了（settle down = 安定下来、认真谈感情）
  - *Example*: All right look, I think it's time for you to settle down.
- **pick a lane**: 选条道 / 选个方向（比喻下定决心，别三心二意）
  - *Example*: Make a choice, pick a lane.
- **swallow our feelings**: 压抑/吞下我们的感情（比喻克制）
  - *Example*: Okay, we-we swallow our feelings.
- **forget it happened**: 当作什么都没发生过
  - *Example*: Here's what we do, we-we forget it happened.
- **pretend that nothing happened**: 假装什么都没发生
  - *Example*: If you wanna pretend that nothing happened, I can try.
- **fix you two up**: 撮合你俩（fix sb up with sb = 给某人介绍对象）
  - *Example*: How 'bout I fix you two up?
- **stepped aside**: 退到一边 / 退出（感情里指成全别人）
  - *Example*: Knowing Joey, he probably just would've just stepped aside.
- **came to me first**: 先来找我（强调先征求意见的尊重）
  - *Example*: You know why? 'Cause you came to me first.
- **went behind my back**: 背着我做（背叛的常用说法）
  - *Example*: You went behind my back! I would never do that to you!
- **you have to tell him**: 你必须告诉他（have to = 必须）
  - *Example*: Well, Chandler, you're gonna have to tell him.
- **I played bad on purpose**: 我是故意弹得很烂的（on purpose = 故意地）
  - *Example*: I played bad on purpose guys.

## 3. 社交与关系

- **the best friends anyone has ever had**: 任何人拥有过的最好的朋友（夸张赞美）
  - *Example*: Oh, you are the best friends anyone has ever had.
- **got a little crush on**: 对…有点暗恋/喜欢（have a crush on = 暗恋）
  - *Example*: I think somebody's got a little crush on Casey.
- **she's into some other guy**: 她迷上别的男的了（be into sb = 喜欢/迷上某人）
  - *Example*: I kinda got the feeling that she's into some other guy.
- **not my girlfriend anymore**: 已经不是我女朋友了（委婉说分手）
  - *Example*: She's not my girlfriend anymore.
- **she broke up with me**: 她跟我分手了（break up with sb）
  - *Example*: Yeah, she broke up with me.
- **I'm in love with her**: 我爱上她了
  - *Example*: Look, I'm sorry! But there's nothing I can do, I think I'm in love with her!
- **you've got you're work cut out for you**: 你有得忙了 / 你得费很大劲（have one's work cut out for one = 面临艰巨任务）
  - *Example*: But hey, listen just so you know, you might have you're work cut out for you.
- **over the line**: 越界 / 过分（cross the line 的形容词形态）
  - *Example*: You're right, I have no excuses! I was totally over the line.
- **screwed over a lot of your friends**: 坑过很多你的朋友（screw over = 坑人、背叛）
  - *Example*: You obviously haven't screwed over a lot of your friends.

## 4. 描述事物

- **as if it appears from nowhere**: 仿佛凭空出现（夸张形容）
  - *Example*: A TV as if it appears from nowhere! That's the dream!
- **I watch it for the articles**: 我看的是它的"文章"（借口看某物其实别有用心，常被拿来调侃）
  - *Example*: Uh yes, but uh, I just watch it for the articles.
- **tainted with your betrayal**: 被你的背叛玷污了（tainted = 受污染的、败坏的）
  - *Example*: Because it's all tainted with your betrayal.
- **all the pieces of my life are falling right into place**: 我人生的所有拼图都完美拼上了（讽刺"万事如意"）
  - *Example*: That all the pieces of my life are falling right into place!
- **the line is a dot to you**: 那条线对你来说只是个点（Joey 夸张 Chandler 越界之远）
  - *Example*: You-you're-you're so far past the line, that you-you can't even see the line! The line is a dot to you!
- **dwarfed by your musical gift**: 被你的音乐才华比得黯然失色（dwarf = 使相形见绌）
  - *Example*: I feel so dwarfed by your musical gift.
- **not even appreciated in his own time**: 连在自己的时代都不被赏识（模仿艺术家死后成名的自嘲）
  - *Example*: Oh my God, he's not even appreciated in his own time.
- **could not get any worse**: 不可能更糟了（表示已经糟到底了）
  - *Example*: Phoebe, his music could not **get** any worse. There are rats in the basement that are hanging themselves.
- **the place has emptied**: 场子都空了
  - *Example*: Phoebe, the place has emptied because of him.

## 5. 实用短句

- **What's up?**: 怎么了？ / 有啥事？
  - *Example*: What's up?
- **Who was that?**: 那是谁？
  - *Example*: Hey-Hey-Hey! Who was that?
- **What happened?**: 发生了什么？ / 出什么事了？
  - *Example*: What happened?
- **You do?**: 真的吗？（反问确认对方的肯定陈述）
  - *Example*: You do?
- **It's me**: 是我（电话里自我介绍的标准用法）
  - *Example*: Hey, it's me.
- **What do you say?**: 你看怎么样？（征求意见）
  - *Example*: Of course, I learned at my aunt's dog grooming shop, but hey, what do you say?
- **I would give anything to...**: 我愿意付出任何代价来…（夸张表达强烈愿望）
  - *Example*: I would give anything to not be appreciated in my own time!
- **What am I gonna do?!**: 我该怎么办？！
  - *Example*: God!! What am I gonna do?!
- **How could I live with myself if...**: 要是…我怎么能原谅自己（夸张自责）
  - *Example*: How could I live with myself if I knew I was depriving the world of your music.
- **Are you still out there?**: 你还在门外吗？
  - *Example*: (Kathy leaves) Are you still out there?
- **I'll be right back**: 我马上回来
  - *Example*: I'll be right back! Okay?

## 6. 剧情词汇

- **Kathy**: Joey 的女友，本集 Chandler 爱上并亲吻了她，是整集主线
- **Casey**: Joey 新约的女孩，Chandler 试图劝 Joey 选她而不是 Kathy
- **Yasmein Bleeth / Yasmine Bleeth**: 美国女演员，*Baywatch*（海滩游侠）主演之一，Chandler 看的就是她
- **Baywatch**: 美剧《海滩游侠》，救生员题材，以泳装美女著称
- **Starbucks**: 星巴克咖啡连锁店，Monica 吐槽 Ross 表演太烂让大家去星巴克
- **The Beetles**: 剧中拼法，实际指 The Beatles（披头士），Phoebe 拿"骑自行车的黑猩猩跟在披头士后面"比喻自己没法在 Ross 之后表演
- **The Sound**: Ross 自称的"个人音色"，他神秘兮兮的键盘演奏——其实只是各种音效拼贴
- **entertainment center**: 家庭娱乐收纳柜，Chandler 买的大件礼物之一，能遥控开关
- **bread maker**: 面包机，Chandler 买给 Joey 的礼物之一，Joey 在里面烤了肉桂葡萄干面包
- **Samboucha / Sambuca**: 意大利茴香酒，Joey 用它调了一杯假"Margarita"
- **Parkway**: 公园大道，美国常见高速公路名，Joey 和 Casey 车抛锚的地方
- **transmission fluid**: 变速箱油，Joey 以为车缺这个，结果整台变速箱都掉出来了
- **dog grooming shop**: 宠物美容店，Kathy 阿姨开的，Kathy 在那儿学会了剪毛——顺手给 Chandler 剪头
- **Central Perk**: 老友记六人组常去的咖啡店
- **Cinnamon raisin loaf**: 肉桂葡萄干面包，Joey 用面包机烤给 Chandler 的，但拒绝吃
- **the line / over the line**: 本集标题关键词"cross a line"——Chandler 吻朋友女友，越过道德底线
