# Friends S03E25 口语表达笔记

## 1. 情绪与反应

- **capable of talking about anything else but...**: 除了……还能不能聊点别的？（表达不耐烦或抱怨）
  - *Example*: I mean is that woman capable of talking about anything else but sex?
- **That is so cool.**: 那太酷了／太棒了。（真诚赞叹）
  - *Example*: That is so cool.
- **big, fat bummerrr**: 真是个天大的坏消息。（Rachel 拉长语调说反话）
  - *Example*: Ohh, big, fat bummerrr.
- **What are you crazy?!**: 你疯了吗？（强烈反对）
  - *Example*: What are you crazy?!
- **Bored and bored!**: 无聊透顶！（用重复制造喜剧效果）
  - *Example*: Bored and bored!
- **that sucks**: 太糟了／真倒霉。（非正式安慰）
  - *Example*: Aww Pheebs, that sucks!
- **What the hell were you thinking?!!**: 你到底是怎么想的？！（愤怒质问）
  - *Example*: What the hell were you thinking?!!
- **getting all flingy**: 激动得手舞足蹈。（Phoebe 自造式口语）
  - *Example*: I'm sorry I'm getting all flingy.
- **felt all floopy**: 感觉慌乱又不自在。（非正式、自造式表达）
  - *Example*: Y'know I wanted to tell you yesterday, but I just, I kinda felt all floopy, and...

## 2. 决策与行动

- **maybe this weekend we could...**: 也许这周末我们可以……（委婉提出建议）
  - *Example*: So maybe this weekend we could go to the beach?
- **if worst comes to worst**: 如果事情坏到最糟的地步。
  - *Example*: Hey now besides, if worst comes to worst, I'll be your boyfriend.
- **we should totally...**: 我们绝对应该……（热情提议，口语）
  - *Example*: Strip poker, we should totally play strip poker.
- **Take it like a man**: 像个男人一样扛住。（带挑衅和玩笑意味）
  - *Example*: Take it like a man, Ross!
- **come up with something fun**: 想出点好玩的事。
  - *Example*: All right, I'm gonna close my eyes and point to someone, and you, whoever I point has to come up with something fun for us to do, and we have to do it.
- **I'm going for a walk.**: 我要去散步。（也可用于结束令人不快的场面）
  - *Example*: I'm going for a walk.
- **there's gotta be something**: 肯定会有某样东西／线索。（非正式 gotta）
  - *Example*: Well, she's out of town so, there's gotta be something in her house that tells me where my Father is.
- **give someone a nudge**: 推某人一把／稍加鼓动。
  - *Example*: That was her idea, I just gave her a nudge.
- **get back together**: 复合。
  - *Example*: What does this mean? What do you, I mean do you wanna, get back together?
- **back away**: 退开；在感情中及时抽身。
  - *Example*: Okay, then you gotta back away, all right?

## 3. 社交与关系

- **boyfriend material**: 适合当男朋友的人。
  - *Example*: What am I not ah, boyfriend material?
- **say we weren't friends**: 假设我们不是朋友。（用 say 引出假设）
  - *Example*: But theoretically y'know, I mean say we weren't friends, say it's a blind date.
- **lose track of somebody**: 和某人失去联系。
  - *Example*: Oh no, I-I'm sorry, I guess we lost track of everybody after high school.
- **flirt with somebody**: 和某人调情。
  - *Example*: He's totally flirting with you too.
- **the happy couple**: 那对甜蜜情侣。（此处带嫉妒和讽刺）
  - *Example*: Well I assume the ah, happy couple isn't up yet.
- **you are soo cool**: 你人真是太好了。（拉长音加强赞美）
  - *Example*: Yeah, thank you Rachel, you are soo cool.
- **the one who ended it**: 主动结束这段关系的人。
  - *Example*: Y'know, hey! You're the one who ended it, remember?
- **can't forgive you for what you did**: 无法原谅你做过的事。
  - *Example*: Ross, I still can't forgive you for what you did, I can't, I just, but sometimes when I'm with you I just, I feel so...
- **get back together**: 重新在一起／复合。
  - *Example*: I'm telling ya, you guys are totally getting back together!
- **Take it from a guy who...**: 听一个……的人的经验之谈。（常带自嘲）
  - *Example*: Take it from a guy who's never had a long term relationship......

## 4. 描述事物

- **kind've an unusual house**: 一栋有点不寻常的房子。（原文口语拼写 kind've）
  - *Example*: Well, yes, it's kind've an unusual house.
- **three beautiful bedrooms and ah, no baths**: 三间漂亮卧室，却没有浴室。（反差式房屋描述）
  - *Example*: It has umm, three beautiful bedrooms and ah, no baths.
- **right there**: 就在那里／近在眼前。
  - *Example*: But y'know, the ocean is right there.
- **Totally familiar.**: 看起来非常眼熟。（简短口语判断）
  - *Example*: Totally familiar.
- **kinda cold**: 有点冷。（非正式 kinda）
  - *Example*: Actually, y'know it's kinda cold, so how about I keep my boxers on, and give you all a peek at the good stuff?
- **the water was sooo great**: 水里感觉太棒了。（拉长音强化感受）
  - *Example*: Oh, the water was sooo great!
- **half of the beach in my hair**: 头发里像带回了半个海滩。（夸张描述）
  - *Example*: I think I brought back half of the beach in my hair.
- **feel all the bones in your skull**: 能摸到头骨的每一块骨头。（描述剃光头后的触感）
  - *Example*: You can feel all the bones in your skull.
- **the moonlight bouncing off her head**: 月光从她头顶反射回来。（夸张取笑光头）
  - *Example*: You can see the moonlight bouncing off her head!
- **not one hair**: 一根头发也没有。（强调完全没有）
  - *Example*: There is not one hair on that head.

## 5. 实用短句

- **hang on a second**: 等一下。
  - *Example*: Ah, oh, hang on a second.
- **think about it, and call me back**: 考虑一下再回电话给我。
  - *Example*: All right, so think about it, and call me back.
- **Take it easy**: 放轻松／别激动。
  - *Example*: Take it easy--if you want, there's cookies on the counter, or, or--sangria!
- **No way!**: 不行／没门儿！
  - *Example*: No way!
- **Get away!**: 走开！
  - *Example*: Get away!
- **Stay away!**: 别靠近！
  - *Example*: Stay away!
- **Fan out!**: 散开！
  - *Example*: Fan out! Fan out!
- **I'll catch up!**: 我会赶上进度的！
  - *Example*: Cool! I'll catch up!
- **help someone out**: 帮某人一把。
  - *Example*: I think I'll help her out.
- **anyone up for...?**: 有人想要……吗？（邀约句型）
  - *Example*: So, anyone up for a midnight dip in the ocean?
- **let me get this straight**: 让我确认一下我有没有理解对。
  - *Example*: So let me get this straight.

## 6. 剧情词汇

- **Phoebe Buffay**: 为寻找生父来到海边，最终发现 Phoebe Sr. 才是自己的生母。
- **Phoebe Sr.**: Lily 的高中好友，也是 Phoebe 的生母；起初隐瞒真相，后来坦白身份。
- **Lily**: Phoebe 一直认作母亲的人；高中时与 Frank、Phoebe Sr. 是好友。
- **Frank Buffay**: Phoebe 寻找的父亲；旧毕业照和冰箱照片推动了寻父线。
- **Ross and Rachel**: 两人在海边旧情复燃并接吻，但 Ross 仍与 Bonnie 交往。
- **Bonnie**: Ross 的女友；受 Rachel 鼓动再次剃光头，引爆三角关系冲突。
- **Chandler and Monica**: 围绕 Chandler 是否有"男友潜质"互相打趣，tag scene 延续模拟约会。
- **Joey**: 不断提议脱衣扑克，还把普通桌游改成脱衣版。
- **Central Perk**: 六人常去的咖啡馆，本集冷开场所在地。
- **Montauk**: 纽约长岛东端的海滨地点，一行人周末旅行的目的地。
- **the beach house**: Phoebe 的客户借出的海边房子；因暴雨和沙灾成为主要场景。
- **B.F.F. / Best Friends Forever**: Phoebe 用来解释母亲年鉴中好友关系的缩写。
- **giant straw hat**: Ross 送给 Rachel 的巨型草帽；既是笑料，也在暴雨中成了遮雨工具。
- **Strip Happy Days Game**: Joey 找不到扑克牌后，把《Happy Days》桌游改造成脱衣游戏。
- **Fonzy, Pinky Tuscadero, Inspiration Point, and Arnold's**: 《Happy Days》相关人物和地点，被桌游卡牌用作"cool points"任务。
- **Michael Jordan**: 美国篮球巨星，以标志性光头成为 Bonnie 剃头后的外貌比喻。
- **Chuck Magioni**: 冰箱照片中的男人；Phoebe Sr. 说他只是自己卖过房子的客户，名字也构成文化梗。
- **M&M**: 巧克力豆品牌；Chandler 用"刚吃了一颗 M&M"荒诞地拒绝下海。
- **photo and appointment book**: Phoebe 寻找身世线索的关键道具；照片引发误会，预约簿促使她闯入屋内查找信息。
- **razor and shaved head**: Rachel 推动 Bonnie 剃头所用的关键道具与结果，直接推动 Ross 与 Rachel 的争吵和告白。
