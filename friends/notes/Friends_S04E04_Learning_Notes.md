# Friends S04E04 口语表达笔记

## 1. 情绪与反应

- **God! If you're gonna cry about it!**: 天哪！你至于为这个哭吗！（不耐烦的语气）
  - *Example*: God! If you're gonna cry about it! (She grabs the box and goes to throw it out.)
- **Whoa-whoa**: 哇哇哇（表示惊讶或阻止，口语高频词）
  - *Example*: Whoa-whoa, Treeger made you cry?
- **Rachel always cries!**: Rachel 老是哭！（夸张抱怨某人动不动就哭）
  - *Example*: Rachel always cries!
- **That's not true!**: 才不是呢！（委屈反驳）
  - *Example*: That's not true! (Starts to cry.)
- **My hero!**: 我的英雄！（夸张调侃，讽刺用法）
  - *Example*: Oh! My hero! What happened?
- **We're doomed.**: 我们完蛋了。（夸张表达绝望）
  - *Example*: We're doomed. Okay, they're gonna take 50 bucks out of our accounts for the rest of our lives.
- **You are soo enjoying this.**: 你明明超享受的。（揭穿对方嘴硬）
  - *Example*: You are soo enjoying this.
- **I don't know how to lead.**: 我不知道怎么领舞。（lead = 在舞蹈中担任引领方）
  - *Example*: I don't know how to lead.
- **Go get 'em!**: 去搞定它！/ 上吧！（鼓励别人去行动，口语固定用法）
  - *Example*: Now you go get that beautiful pig! Oink!
- **No! And I'm not gonna be, so you can save your little speech.**: 不是！我也不打算加入，你那套说辞就省省吧。（直接拒绝推销）
  - *Example*: No! And I'm not gonna be, so you can save your little speech.

## 2. 决策与行动

- **it was time**: 是时候了（早该做某事了）
  - *Example*: Yeah, it was time. The old condom ring in the leather just doesn't say 'cool' anymore.
- **teach that guy a lesson**: 给那人一个教训（口语：收拾某人）
  - *Example*: I'm gonna go down there and teach that guy a lesson.
- **school is in session**: 开课了/好戏上场了（幽默：表示要动真格了）
  - *Example*: All right that's it, school is in session! (Exits and slams the door.)
- **I don't need this grief**: 我不想惹这麻烦（grief = 麻烦、烦恼，口语）
  - *Example*: I've been a nice guy up until now, but uh, I don't need this grief.
- **suck up to him**: 拍他马屁 / 讨好他
  - *Example*: Now Joey, you go down there and you suck up to him.
- **suck like you've never sucked before**: 拼命拍马屁，拍到你从没拍过那么狠的程度（夸张指令）
  - *Example*: I mean you suck like you've never sucked before!
- **hold your hand**: 牵你的手（比喻：陪你一起去、给你壮胆）
  - *Example*: You need me to go down there with you and hold your hand?
- **on your own**: 独自 / 靠自己
  - *Example*: So you're strong enough to face her on your own?
- **get this over with**: 赶紧把这事了结（不想拖延）
  - *Example*: All right, I'm here, let's ahh, get this over with.
- **follow my lead**: 跟着我的节奏来 / 听我的
  - *Example*: Okay ahh, well, just ahh, follow my lead.
- **close our accounts**: 注销账户（银行语境）
  - *Example*: We'd like to close our accounts.
- **cut them off at the source**: 从源头切断（比喻：从根本上解决问题）
  - *Example*: We could go to the bank, close our accounts and cut them off at the source.

## 3. 社交与关系

- **make someone cry**: 把某人弄哭了
  - *Example*: Now you made my friend, Rachel, cry.
- **have someone evicted**: 把某人赶出去（evict = 驱逐、赶出租房）
  - *Example*: He said that he wasn't gonna apologize because you guys are living here illegally, so instead what he's gonna do is have you evicted.
- **storm out**: 怒气冲冲地冲出去
  - *Example*: Why don't you tell me something I don't know! (He storms out.)
- **against my oath**: 违反我的誓言（职业操守语境）
  - *Example*: And it's against my oath as a masseuse.
- **fooling around with clients**: 跟客户搞暧昧 / 乱来
  - *Example*: Mrs. Potter fires people for fooling around with clients.
- **owe someone big time**: 欠某人大人情
  - *Example*: Ah-ha-ha, you guys owe me big time.
- **they make you go all the way down there**: 他们让你大老远亲自跑一趟（抱怨流程繁琐）
  - *Example*: They make you go all the way down there! Then they use all of these phrases and peppiness to try and confuse you!
- **try and confuse you**: 想方设法把你绕晕（try and = try to，口语省略）
  - *Example*: Then they use all of these phrases and peppiness to try and confuse you!
- **go for someone**: 看上某人 / 接受某人（ romantically ）
  - *Example*: I'm sure as hell a dancer, it's no use Marge will never go for me.
- **bank buddies**: 银行伙伴（讽刺：一起用同一家银行的朋友）
  - *Example*: Aww, man, now we won't be bank buddies!

## 4. 描述事物

- **the old condom ring in the leather just doesn't say 'cool' anymore**: 皮革上那圈避孕套的印子已经不"酷"了（say = 传达某种印象）
  - *Example*: Yeah, it was time. The old condom ring in the leather just doesn't say 'cool' anymore.
- **a big potato with arms, and legs, and a head**: 一个长了胳膊腿和脑袋的大土豆（自嘲身材笨拙）
  - *Example*: I'm just a big potato with arms, and legs, and a head.
- **sure as hell**: 肯定是 / 绝对是（口语强调）
  - *Example*: I'm sure as hell a dancer, it's no use Marge will never go for me.
- **flabby gut and saggy man breasts**: 松垮的肚子和下垂的男人胸（自嘲不锻炼的体型）
  - *Example*: I want a flabby gut and saggy man breasts!
- **washboard stomach and rock hard pecs**: 搓衣板腹肌和岩石般坚硬的胸肌（健身房推销话术）
  - *Example*: Hey, don't you want a washboard stomach and rock hard pecs?
- **lycra spandex covered gym treat**: 穿着莱卡弹性纤维的健身房尤物（treat = 令人心动的人/物）
  - *Example*: She's like this lycra spandex covered gym…treat.
- **not prison lingo, is it?**: 不是监狱黑话吧？（lingo = 行话/术语，Joey 担心"跳舞搭档"有隐含义）
  - *Example*: That's not, prison lingo, is it?
- **some kind of like girly dance**: 某种娘们儿兮兮的舞（girly = 女孩气的，贬义口语）
  - *Example*: Hey-hey, hold on, this isn't some kind of like girly dance.

## 5. 实用短句

- **Yeah, it was time.**: 对，早该换了。
  - *Example*: Yeah, it was time. The old condom ring in the leather just doesn't say 'cool' anymore.
- **Have you ever taken out the trash?**: 你倒过垃圾吗？（质问语气）
  - *Example*: Have you ever taken out the trash? (Hands her the garbage.)
- **I told you not to go down there!**: 我跟你说过了别下去！（事后埋怨）
  - *Example*: I told you not to go down there!
- **Why don't you tell me something I don't know!**: 你能说点我不知道的吗！（反讽：这我早知道了）
  - *Example*: Why don't you tell me something I don't know!
- **What are you doing?**: 你在干嘛？（质问/惊讶）
  - *Example*: What are you doing?
- **All right that's it**: 好了，够了（表示忍无可忍要采取行动）
  - *Example*: All right that's it, school is in session!
- **I wanna quit the gym.**: 我想退健身房。（wanna = want to，口语缩略）
  - *Example*: I wanna quit the gym.
- **Could you come here for a second?**: 你能过来一下吗？（叫人过来的礼貌说法）
  - *Example*: Could you come here for a second?
- **What was that?**: 那是什么？（没听清或没看懂时问）
  - *Example*: What was that?
- **Where'd you get that?**: 你从哪搞来的？（where'd = where did）
  - *Example*: So, uh, where'd you get that?

## 6. 剧情词汇

- **Mr. Treeger**: 公寓楼管（superintendent），粗犷但内心想学跳舞参加管理员舞会
- **Marge**: 另一位女楼管（lady super），Treeger 暗恋的对象，想在 Super Ball 上给她留下好印象
- **Super Ball**: 管理员年度舞会（superintendent's dance），Treeger 要在 Marge 面前展示舞技
- **Rick**: Phoebe 的按摩客户，Greenpeace 环保人士，被 Phoebe 咬了一口
- **Mrs. Potter**: Phoebe 的按摩店老板，禁止员工跟客户乱搞
- **Maria**: 健身房女员工，穿 spandex 的美女，专门负责让退会员的人动摇
- **Healing Hands Inc.**: Phoebe 工作的按摩店全称
- **Greenpeace**: 绿色和平组织，Rick 曾参加其 16 小时静坐抗议
- **Boy Scouts**: 童子军，Phoebe 的"always be prepared"誓言来自童子军而非按摩师誓言
- **Rent Stabilization Act of 1968**: 1968年纽约租金稳定法，Treeger 用这条法律威胁 Monica 是非法转租奶奶的公寓
- **pademarie**: 芭蕾术语 pas de marée（小步舞动作），Joey 不自觉蹦出来的专业词汇，暴露他其实很享受跳舞
- **Swedish spa**: 瑞典式水疗（按摩类服务），健身房用来诱惑 Chandler 不退出
- ***Cats***: 音乐剧《猫》，Rachel 小时候爸爸请演员给她唱生日歌的回忆（体现她富裕的成长背景）
- **Central Perk**: 六人组常去的咖啡店，本集多场景发生地
- **the chick and the duck**: Joey 和 Chandler 养的宠物鸡和鸭，Joey 用它们当借口不让 Treeger 赶人
