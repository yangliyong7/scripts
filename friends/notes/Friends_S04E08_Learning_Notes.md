# Friends S04E08 Learning Notes

## 1. 情绪与反应

- **can't stand (to do sth)**: 无法忍受（做某事）；表示极度反感或不耐烦
  - *Example*: I know you can't stand to be in the same room as me.

- **it really kills**: 疼得要命（口语夸张表达疼痛）
  - *Example*: Oh, y'know what, I can't, it really kills.

- **Ewwwww!**: 恶心！（表示强烈的反感或不适，拖长音加强语气）
  - *Example*: Ewwwww!! Ew! Ew! Ew!

- **it's sick / twisted**: 变态的、不正常的（口语中对不合常理的事的评价，带夸张）
  - *Example*: I know, it's sick.

- **This sucks!**: 太糟了！（俚语，表示对现状非常不满）
  - *Example*: All right this sucks! I already got her this briefcase.

- **Oh my God!**: 天哪！（惊讶、震惊、兴奋皆可用）
  - *Example*: Oh my God! You actually exchanged it!

- **I'm honored!**: 我很荣幸！（可以是真诚的也可以是反讽的）
  - *Example*: Wow! I'm honored! And y'know what I'm gonna do as my first act as your best friend?

- **break the tension**: 打破尴尬 / 缓和紧张气氛
  - *Example*: You can't tell, but I'm trying to break the tension by mooning you guys!

## 2. 决策与行动

- **hear someone out**: 听某人把话说完（给对方解释的机会）
  - *Example*: Don't you think you could at least hear the guy out?

- **work it out**: 解决问题、把事情谈开
  - *Example*: At first I thought we could talk about this y'know, work it out.

- **push for someone**: 替某人说话、力挺某人
  - *Example*: I don't know why you're pushing for him so hard.

- **take this seriously**: 认真对待这件事
  - *Example*: So are you gonna start taking this thing seriously?

- **get out of there**: 从那里出来（字面）；也可指离开困境
  - *Example*: Come on, get out of there! Get out of there!

- **catch her**: 追上她（字面义：跑着去追到那个人）
  - *Example*: He can still catch her!

- **exchange (a gift)**: 退换（礼物）；把买来的东西拿回商店换成别的或换成 store credit
  - *Example*: She exchanges every gift she ever gets.

- **send back**: 退回、寄还（购买的东西）
  - *Example*: I'm sending back all this stuff that Chandler bought out of guilt.

## 3. 社交与关系

- **secret Santa**: 秘密圣诞老人（一种抽签互赠礼物的聚会游戏，每人只买一份）
  - *Example*: This year maybe we could do secret Santa, and then we each only buy one gift.

- **Who gets whom**: "谁抽到谁"（Ross 纠正语法：宾格应用 whom，其他人觉得他书呆子气）
  - *Example*: Who gets whom. I don't know why I do that.

- **wanna trade / switch**: 想交换（这里指交换 secret Santa 的对象）
  - *Example*: I was wondering if you wanna switch.

- **best friend**: 最好的朋友（本集核心概念，Joey 和 Chandler 的友情考验）
  - *Example*: No, was my best friend.

- **come between (two people)**: 介入/破坏两人之间的关系
  - *Example*: I don't wanna be someone who comes between two best friends.

- **see each other**: 交往、约会（恋爱关系的委婉说法）
  - *Example*: I don't think we can see each other anymore.

- **screw up**: 搞砸了（俚语，承认自己犯了错）
  - *Example*: I know what it is to be a friend, I just-I just screwed up!

- **Don't do the crime if you can't do the time**: 做了亏心事就得承担后果（口语化的谚语/俗语）
  - *Example*: Hey! Don't do the crime if you can't do the time.

## 4. 描述事物

- **threefold / three fold**: 三重的、有三个层面的
  - *Example*: The meaning of the box is three fold.

- **devoid of (sentiment)**: 完全缺乏（某种品质或情感），略正式
  - *Example*: I'm not completely devoid of sentiment, see I have feelings.

- **on-call (doctor)**: 值班的（医生）；随叫随到的
  - *Example*: He's out of town, but does she want to see the on-call doctor?

- **out of guilt**: 出于内疚（解释做某事的动机）
  - *Example*: I'm sending back all this stuff that Chandler bought out of guilt.

- **girlie**: 女性化的、偏女生气质的（非正式，略带调侃）
  - *Example*: It's kind've a girlie briefcase.

- **a scratch on your cornea**: 角膜上有个划痕（cornea = 角膜）
  - *Example*: You've got a little scratch on your cornea.

- **for the best**: （虽然现在痛苦但）也许这样是最好的结果
  - *Example*: This is probably for the best.

## 5. 实用短句

- **How cute is …!**: ……多帅/多可爱啊！（感叹句式，表赞美）
  - *Example*: Oh my God! How cute is the on-call doctor?

- **by any chance**: 不知道是否恰好……（礼貌询问的开头）
  - *Example*: Hey, by any chance did either of pick uh Rachel for your secret Santa?

- **get a lot of use out of**: 能经常用到、很实用
  - *Example*: Isn't it better that I exchanged it for something that I enjoy and that I can get a lot of use out of?

- **What is the matter with you?!**: 你怎么回事！你有毛病吗！（质问对方反常行为）
  - *Example*: Would you stop?! What is the matter with you?!

- **I'll get it!**: 我来开门！/ 我来接电话！（有人敲门或电话响时抢着去的说法）
  - *Example*: I'll get it! I'll get it! I'll get it!

- **Speed dial 7**: 快速拨号7号键（说明对那个号码非常熟悉，早就设好了快捷键）
  - *Example*: Like I remember his office number! Speed dial 7.

- **Here, let me help**: 来，我帮你（主动帮忙时的自然开头）
  - *Example*: Here, let me help.

- **get a room**: 开个房间去吧（对当众秀恩爱的情侣的打趣）
  - *Example*: All right, get a room.

- **See you around**: 回见吧 / 后会有期（带点距离感或赌气的告别）
  - *Example*: See you around!

## 6. 剧情词汇

- **Kathy**: Chandler 当时的女友，原本是 Joey 的女朋友。Chandler 背着 Joey 亲了她，导致本集两人关系破裂。
- **the box**: 一个大木箱，Chandler 自愿钻进去待着以证明他重视和 Joey 的友谊。本集标题来源，也是全集最核心的视觉笑点。
- **Richard Burke**: Monica 的前男友，是个眼科医生，比她大很多。本集 Monica 眼睛受伤去看病时发现值班的竟然是 Richard 的儿子 Tim。
- **Tim / Dr. Timothy Burke**: Richard 的儿子，年轻帅气的眼科医生。Monica 请他来感恩节晚餐，但亲吻时发现跟他爸感觉一模一样，两人都崩溃了。
- **Thanksgiving**: 感恩节，本集的时间背景。大家在 Monica 家聚餐，同时进行 secret Santa 抽签。
- **Chanukah**: 犹太光明节（Ross 是犹太人，所以他另外给 Monica 准备 Chanukah 礼物）。
- **92.3 WXRK / K-Rock**: 纽约一家真实存在的摇滚电台，Chandler 假装电台打电话来骗 Joey 接听。
- **the entertainment unit**: 之前被抢劫那集里 Joey 被锁在里面的大型娱乐柜，本集 Chandler 提到它引出"进箱子"的点子。
- ***The Dairy Queen***: 美国连锁冰淇淋快餐店，Tim 回忆小时候最后一次见 Monica 时她站在 DQ 门口（暗示她当时还是个贪吃的胖女孩）。
- **store credit**: 商店积分/退货额度（不是退现金，而是换成店内消费额度）。Rachel 把 Ross 送的金项链退了换成 credit，成为本集 B 线笑点。
- **Greek tragedy**: 希腊悲剧（Rachel 用来形容 Monica 约会前男友的儿子这件事有多离谱——像古希腊悲剧里的乱伦剧情）。
- **walk the plank**: 走跳板（海盗惩罚俘虏的方式，让人走到船边木板尽头掉进海里）。Monica 戴上眼罩后开始扮海盗说话。
