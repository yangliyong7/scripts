# Friends S05E09 口语表达笔记

## 1. 情绪与反应

- **Ew-eww!!**: 恶心死了（发现沙发上有内裤）。
  - *Example*: **Ew-eww!!** **Undies**!
- **MY SANDWICH!!!!!!**: 名场面怒吼（老板承认吃了三明治）。
  - *Example*: **MY SANDWICH?!!!** / **MY SANDWICH!!!!!!**
- **Just a sandwich?**: 只是个三明治？（Ross 暴怒：人生低谷只剩这个）。
  - *Example*: Ross, **it's just a sandwich**! — **Just a sandwich?**
- **Someone ate my sandwich**: 有人吃了我的三明治（感恩节剩菜）。
  - *Example*: **Someone at work ate my sandwich**!
- **I can't believe someone ate it**: 不敢相信有人吃了（反复强调）。
  - *Example*: I still **can't believe someone ate it**!!
- **On account of my rage**: 因为我的暴怒（被迫看心理医生）。
  - *Example*: On **account of my rage**.
- **Out of control**: 失控了（Chandler 吐槽 Ross 的怒气）。
  - *Example*: Which I may say, right now, is **out of control**.
- **You are a bad ass**: 你真狠（夸 Phoebe 护食便签）。
  - *Example*: Phoebe, **you are a bad ass**!
- **Oh my God!**: 震惊（Rachel 发现裸体照、录像机等）。
  - *Example*: **Oh my God!** That's Monica!!

## 2. 决策与行动

- **Covering for you two**: 一直帮你们打掩护（Joey 忍无可忍）。
  - *Example*: I'm tired of **covering for you two**!
- **Get tough with people**: 对人强硬就能要什么有什么（Ross 学歪了）。
  - *Example*: You **get tough with people** you can get anything you want.
- **Give me that coffee! Now!**: Ross 学狠，结果被 Joey 掀翻沙发。
  - *Example*: Hey Tribbiani, **give me that coffee! Now!**
- **Scare people off**: 把人吓跑才能保住吃的（Phoebe 街头经验）。
  - *Example*: You gotta **scare people off**. I learned that living on the street.
- **Thrown my sandwich away**: 老板还把大部分扔垃圾桶。
  - *Example*: I-I had to **throw most of it away**.
- **Take a leave of absence**: 被迫休假（Ross 对老板吼叫后）。
  - *Example*: They're making you **take a leave of absence**.
- **Come out of the non-gay closet**: 出柜变「直」——逼 Monica/Chandler 公开恋情。
  - *Example*: Get ready to **come out of the non-gay closet**!
- **Keep it as a souvenir**: 留着当纪念品（Monica 配合圆谎内裤）。
  - *Example*: I wanted to keep it **as a souvenir**.

## 3. 社交与关系

- **Chandler? A word.**: 钱德勒，跟你说句话（私下算账暗号）。
  - *Example*: **Chandler? A word.**
- **We're so bad at relationships**: 我们谈恋爱太烂，所以才要保密。
  - *Example*: We're just so **bad at relationships**.
- **You do it with me once**: Joey 开玩笑：帮瞒可以，你也得跟我睡一次。
  - *Example*: But, you **do it with me once**.
- **Still wondering?**: 还在等我问你约会吗？（Joey 装傻，其实已在约会）。
  - *Example*: I've been wondering if you were going to ask me out. — So you uh, **still wondering**?
- **You're smart. I like that.**: 你很聪明，我喜欢（Joey 撩 Cynthia）。
  - *Example*: **You're smart. I like that.**
- **I slept with Monica**: 终极谎话——伦敦一夜情，解释一切暧昧证据。
  - *Example*: **I slept with Monica**. …we just did it once uh, in London.
- **Explain it with the truth**: 也可以用真相解释（Joey 差点说漏）。
  - *Example*: You can also explain it with **the truth**.
- **Could not be enticed**: 可惜诱惑不了我（Joey 撇清录像机）。
  - *Example*: Sadly I **could not be enticed**.
- **I'm Monica. I'm disgusting.**: Monica 学 Joey 经典句式自黑收尾。
  - *Example*: **I'm Monica. I'm disgusting.** I stalk guys and keep their underpants.

## 4. 描述事物

- **Undies / tighty-whiteys**: 内裤；Joey 吐槽 Chandler 穿「八岁小孩」白内裤。
  - *Example*: **Undies**! / **Tighty-whiteys**! What are you, 8?
- **Moist Maker**: Monica 秘方：三明治中间多一片浸肉汁的面包。
  - *Example*: An extra slice of gravy soaked bread in the middle; I call it the **Moist Maker**.
- **Mental Geller**: 同事给 Ross 起的狠昵称（Phoebe 恐吓便签见效后）。
  - *Example*: A guy called me mental! **Mental Geller**.
- **Knock-knock joke note**: Chandler 在便签上写烂笑话护食，没用。
  - *Example*: **Knock-knock.** Who's there? Ross Geller's lunch…
- **Wet Pants Geller**: Ross 高中绰号「湿裤盖勒」（饮水机事故）。
  - *Example*: The best you got in high school was **Wet Pants Geller**.
- **Low-budget adult films**: 低成本成人片（Joey 背锅掩护 Chandler/Monica）。
  - *Example*: I make **low-budget adult films**.
- **Goodie-goodie**: 乖乖女（Rachel 催 Phoebe 别那么较真）。
  - *Example*: Don't be such a **goodie-goodie**!
- **Jane Eyre is a cyborg**: 报复梗——Phoebe 胡扯简·爱是机器人。
  - *Example*: Jane Eyre…you'd think she's a woman, but she's not. She's a **cyborg**.
- **Jane Eyre to Robocop**: 把《简·爱》和《铁甲威龙》比（全班社死）。
  - *Example*: Comparing Jane Eyre to **Robocop**.
- **Repeating what I'm saying**: 学舌梗——Phoebe 与老师互相复读。
  - *Example*: Are you just **repeating what I'm saying**?
- **Wildness of the mores**: 礼教狂野的一面（Rachel 瞎掰装读过）。
  - *Example*: The **wildness of the mores**, which…is mirrored in Heathcliff's character.
- **Secret peephole**: Rachel 以为 Joey 装窥视孔偷窥。
  - *Example*: Joey has got a secret **peephole**!
- **Eats chicken and looks at them**: 边吃炸鸡边看裸照（Rachel 恶心脑补）。
  - *Example*: He **eats chicken and looks at them**!

## 5. 实用短句

- **I'm out**: 我不猜了→放弃/退出（猜 Phoebe 坐啥，Chandler 猜不出）。
  - *Example*: Top of the world? Dock of the bay? …**I'm out**.
- **Top of the world? Dock of the bay?**: 坐在世界之巅？海湾码头？→歌名梗瞎猜她坐啥。
  - *Example*: **Top of the world? Dock of the bay?**
- **One thing led to another**: 一件事引发另一件→不知不觉发展成…（圆谎连锁：剃刀→刮腿毛）。
  - *Example*: I said I was playing a woman in a play. And **one thing led to another** and…
- **Keep your mitts off my grub**: 手别碰我的吃的→别动我东西（mitts=手，grub=吃的）。
  - *Example*: Stuff like uh, "**Keep your mitts off my grub**?"
- **The good Joey name**: 好名声→「乔伊这名儿」被你们糟蹋（reputation）。
  - *Example*: The **good Joey name** is being dragged through the mud here!
- **Dragged through the mud**: 拖进泥里→名声被败坏。
  - *Example*: The good Joey name is being **dragged through the mud** here!
- **What's wrong buddy?**: 怎么了哥们（Joey 对沮丧的 Ross）。
  - *Example*: **What's wrong buddy?**
- **Still can't let that one go**: 还是放不下那件事（Carol 女同等旧伤）。
  - *Example*: You **still can't let that one go**, can you?
- **Get worked up**: 又激动起来→又开始发火（看心理医生时又上火）。
  - *Example*: When the psychiatrist told me… I started to **get worked up** again.
- **On account of**: 因为/由于→稍正式表原因（非「账户上」）。
  - *Example*: I saw a psychiatrist at work today. **On account of** my rage.
- **Adult film class**: 成人电影课（Chandler 胡编拍作业借口）。
  - *Example*: You had to make an adult film for your **adult film class**.
- **You completely stole my answer!**: 你完全抢我答案！
  - *Example*: **You completely stole my answer**!
- **Playing a woman in a play**: 戏里演女人（Joey 拿 Monica 剃刀圆谎）。
  - *Example*: I was **playing a woman in a play**.
- **Video tape us on the first date**: 首约就拍性爱录像（Cynthia 误会吓跑）。
  - *Example*: You thought you were going to **video tape us having sex on the first date**!
- **Only eating the skin**: 只吃鸡皮，鸡肉大家随便拿（Joey 结尾）。
  - *Example*: I'm **only eating the skin**, so the chicken's up for grabs.
- **Pill / tranquilizer**: 镇静药（Ross 吃药后佛系：不在乎三明治了）。
  - *Example*: He gave me a **pill** for it. …he offered me a **tranquilizer**.
