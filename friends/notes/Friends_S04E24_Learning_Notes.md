# Friends S04E24 口语表达笔记

## 1. 情绪与反应
- **I like you less!**: 我更讨厌你了→Phoebe 厌恶疗法拍头后 Rachel 的回怼。
  - *Example*: Well, **I like you less**!
- **horrible, horrible person**: 糟糕透顶的人（飞机上乘客骂 Rachel 要去毁婚礼）。
  - *Example*: You are a **horrible, horrible person**.
- **I can't imagine why**: 我无法想象为什么→反讽「难怪呢」（柜台员听 Rachel 大闹）。
  - *Example*: He is going to marry that other girl!!! — **I can't imagine why**.
- **never going to get married**: 我永远嫁不出去了（Monica 被当成 Ross 妈之后崩溃）。
  - *Example*: Oh, my mother's right. **I'm never going to get married**.
- **NO GRANDCHILDREN!**: 别想抱孙子了！（Ross 吼双方父母别吵）。
  - *Example*: If I hear one more word. **NO GRANDCHILDREN**! That's right!!
- **This is worse than when he married the lesbian**: 比他娶女同性恋那次还糟（Judy 评婚礼）。
  - *Example*: **This is worse than when he married the lesbian**.

## 2. 决策与行动
- **aversion therapy**: 厌恶疗法（Phoebe 用照片+打头帮 Rachel 忘掉 Ross）。
  - *Example*: Let's try some uh, **aversion therapy**.
- **swing around and come and pick me up**: 让飞机掉头回来接我（Rachel 对柜台员的无理要求）。
  - *Example*: Tell them to **swing around and come and pick me up**.
- **pulled away from the gate**: 已经离开登机口→飞机推离廊桥，上不去了。
  - *Example*: That plane has already **pulled away from the gate**.
- **step aside**: 请让开→柜台员请 Rachel 靠边站。
  - *Example*: I'm going to have to ask you to **step aside**, Miss.
- **Rachel coming. Do...Something**: Rachel 来了。做……点什么（Joey 的便条作战计划）。
  - *Example*: Rachel coming. **Do...Something**.
- **ruin the wedding**: 毁了婚礼（Phoebe 催 Joey 拦 Rachel）。
  - *Example*: You have to stop her!! She's going to **ruin the wedding**!!
- **Shall I go on?**: 要我继续吗？→牧师问仪式是否继续（Ross 喊错名后）。
  - *Example*: Uhh...**Shall I go on**?
- **We'd better start again**: 最好重新开始（牧师让 Ross 重念誓词）。
  - *Example*: I think we'd **better start again**.
- **You may kiss the bride**: 你可以亲吻新娘（婚礼口令）。
  - *Example*: **You may kiss the bride**.
- **Just keep smiling**: 保持微笑就行（Emily 挽 Ross 离场时冷冷说）。
  - *Example*: **Just keep smiling**.

## 3. 社交与关系
- **you were on a break**: 你们当时分手了（陌生乘客站 Ross，全网梗）。
  - *Example*: It seems to be perfectly clear that you **were on a break**.
- **rebound thing from me**: 因为我才分的「反弹恋情」→Rachel 坚称 Emily 不算真爱。
  - *Example*: It's just a **rebound thing from me**.... You'll see!
- **happiest day of his life**: 他一生中最幸福的一天→你要去毁掉（乘客骂 Rachel）。
  - *Example*: You're about to ruin the **happiest day of his life**.
- **number one girl**: 头号女孩→Joey 哄 Phoebe「你仍是第一」。
  - *Example*: You know you're still my **number one girl**.
- **Talk New York to me again**: 再跟我讲讲纽约嘛（伴娘 Felicity 撩 Joey）。
  - *Example*: **Talk New York to me again**.
- **Fuggetaboutit**: 纽约口音「别想了/算了」（Joey 撩妹表演）。
  - *Example*: (In a New York accent.) **Fuggetaboutit**. How you doin?
- **What we did last night was... stupid**: 昨晚那事……太蠢了（Chandler/Monica 婚礼过道私语）。
  - *Example*: **What we did last night was....** — **Stupid**.
- **I'm coming over tonight though, right?**: 我今晚还过去吧？（两人假装没事）。
  - *Example*: **I'm coming over tonight though, right?** — Oh yeah. Definitely.
- **Who wouldn't want you?**: 谁不要你啊（Chandler 安慰 Monica）。
  - *Example*: Ahh, you know what? That is....**Who wouldn't want you**?
- **Do you think he knew I was here?**: 你觉得他发现我在这吗？（Monica 躲 Ross 床后）。
  - *Example*: **Do you think he knew I was here**?

## 4. 描述事物
- **bond everlasting**: 永恒的结合（婚礼誓词）。
  - *Example*: A symbol of your **bond everlasting**.
- **the whole bloody lot**: 整笔费用（英式；Waltham 父让 Geller 买单）。
  - *Example*: You're paying for **the whole bloody lot**.
- **Take thee, EM-I-LY**: 娶你，E-M-I-L-Y（牧师拼读防 Ross 再喊错）。
  - *Example*: Take thee, **EM-I-LY**...
- **third degree**: 连珠炮审问→Joey 嫌 Chandler 追问像审讯。
  - *Example*: What's with the **third degree**?! Why don't you just **shine a light in my eyes**?!
- **big hole at the back of the building**: 大楼后面的大洞→Joey 让 Monica 盯住后门防 Rachel 闯入。
  - *Example*: You watch that **big hole at the back of the building**.

## 5. 实用短句
- **I'm getting married today!!**: 我今天要结婚啦！！（Ross 冲进 Chandler 房间）。
  - *Example*: **I'm getting married today!!** Whoo-hoo!!
- **Take thee, Rachel / Emily**: 娶你为妻→Ross 脱口 Rachel 全场石化（季终 cliffhanger）。
  - *Example*: **Take thee, Rachel**...(He realises.) **Emily**. Emily.
- **Like there'd be anybody else**: 还能有谁啊→自嘲掩饰。
  - *Example*: Take thee, Emily. (Chuckles) **Like there'd be anybody else**.
- **Do you think I should go up there?**: 你觉得我该上去吗？（Rachel 听 Ross 喊自己名）。
  - *Example*: He-he said Rachel, right? **Do you think I should go up there**?
- **It could've been worse, he could've shot her**: 本来可以更糟（Chandler 黑色幽默）。
  - *Example*: **It could've been worse, he could've shot her**.
- **You want a piece of me?**: 想跟我干一架？（Waltham 父挑衅 Geller）。
  - *Example*: **You want a piece of me**, sir?
- **slip you one**: 偷偷给你一片药→给你下镇静剂。
  - *Example*: Perhaps **slip you one**.
- **I'm not leaving here until you call that plane back**: 不叫回飞机我就不走。
  - *Example*: **I am not leaving here, until you call that plane back**!!
- **declare them husband and wife**: 宣布你们结为夫妻（Uncut 婚礼续办）。
  - *Example*: Pleasure to **declare them husband and wife**.
- **Could you not look?**: 你能不能别看我？（Monica 下床时）。
  - *Example*: **Could you not look?** — I don't want to look.
- **let some of them go by**: 有些愿望放一放吧→Monica 怼 Judy 别催婚。
  - *Example*: **You can let some of them go by**!

## 6. 剧情词汇
- **S04E24 / Ross's Wedding Part II**: 伦敦婚礼日；Rachel 飞去伦敦
- **Take thee, Rachel**: Ross 婚礼口误，第四季 cliffhanger
- **EM-I-LY**: 牧师重念誓词时拼读防口误
- **Felicity**: 伴娘；Joey「Talk New York」线
- **Virgin Atlantic flight**: Rachel 飞往伦敦的航班
- **Westminster Abbey / chapel**: 婚礼教堂场景
- **Bond everlasting**: 婚礼誓词关键词
- **Uncut extension**: 加长版含婚礼办完、婚宴争吵等 Part II 删减镜头
