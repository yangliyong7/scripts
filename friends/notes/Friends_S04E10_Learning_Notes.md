# Friends S04E10 口语表达笔记

## 1. 情绪与反应

- **I hate being alone this time of year**: 我最讨厌这个季节一个人（节日季形单影只的孤独感）。
  - *Example*: I hate being alone this time of year! Next thing you know it'll be Valentine's Day, then my birthday, then bang!
- **at my mercy**: 完全在我的掌控之中、任我摆布（merc＝怜悯，字面"只能指望我发慈悲"）。
  - *Example*: I sat near the door, so she'd have to pass by me if she wanted to switch cars. She was totally at my mercy.
- **That joke would've killed in Albany**: 这笑话在奥尔巴尼肯定炸场（kill＝大获成功；其实冷场了，自嘲找台阶）。
  - *Example*: How can she be great if she's from Poughkeepsie? (laughs, at they all look at him) That joke would've killed in Albany.
- **who's stupid now**: 现在谁傻？（得逞后的得意反击）。
  - *Example*: Done! I did it! Heh, who's stupid now?
- **I can't take this anymore**: 我再也受不了了。
  - *Example*: They baked it. I can't take this anymore. I'm gonna call a meeting tonight, I'm gonna fire you tonight.
- **What the hell happened?!**: 到底怎么回事？！（又气又急的质问）。
  - *Example*: What the hell happened?!
- **this sucks**: 这糟透了（口语吐槽万能句）。
  - *Example*: See just I'm right back where I started! Aww, this sucks! Being alone, sucks!

## 2. 决策与行动

- **set you up**: 给你介绍对象、牵线搭桥。
  - *Example*: Y'know what? There's some nice guys at my office, do you want me to set you up?
- **go out of my way**: 特意费心去做（字面"绕出我的路"）。
  - *Example*: I have a girlfriend, I'm happy. So, I no longer feel the need to go out of my way to stop others from being happy.
- **make headway**: 有进展、见到起色（headway＝前进）。
  - *Example*: I thought I was making headway, everyone was smiling at me all day.
- **pick on**: 欺负、刁难、找茬。
  - *Example*: I have not been picked on this much since kindergarten and they had to bring in someone from junior high to do the see-saw with me.
- **not good at confrontation**: 不擅长正面冲突、不会跟人撕破脸。
  - *Example*: I would love too, but I can't! I mean I just can't, you know that I'm not good at confrontation.
- **end them both**: 把两段关系都了结（干脆利落的建议）。
  - *Example*: You take a train up to Poughkeepsie and break up with her, and on your way back you break up with uptown. And then by the time you get home tonight, you're done!
- **gain their trust**: 赢得他们的信任。
  - *Example*: You don't fire me, instead I stay here, I gain their trust, and they'll start listening to all the nice things I've been saying about you.

## 3. 社交与关系

- **a fling**: 短暂的风流、不图长久的露水情缘（flung 是 fling 的变体）。
  - *Example*: It doesn't even have to be a big relationship, y'know, just like a fling would be great. / Well, believe me, it's been a long time since I've been flung.
- **play the field**: 多接触几个人、不急着定下来（字面"在整个场子里打转"）。
  - *Example*: Y'know what, I'm gonna uh, play the field just a little more.
- **turn into something serious**: 发展成认真的关系。
  - *Example*: Patrick and I had such a great time last night! I mean I think this could maybe turn into something serious.
- **get the wrong idea**: 误会、想歪（往坏处或暧昧处理解）。
  - *Example*: Oh, between you telling him that I wanted to have a fling and me putting out on the first date—oh, he's so gonna get the wrong idea.
- **put out**: （俚语）答应发生关系（常指不该太早时）。
  - *Example*: Between you telling him that I wanted to have a fling and me putting out on the first date...
- **like father, like son**: 有其父必有其子（Chandler 自夸和儿子眼光一样好）。
  - *Example*: Rachel: Chandler!! You have the best taste in men! Chandler: Well, like father, like son.
- **a great catch**: 很抢手的对象（catch＝值得追的人）。
  - *Example*: Well, y'know, you're-you're gonna meet somebody! You're a great catch!
- **We had a deal**: 我们有约在先、说好的。
  - *Example*: Joey, we had a deal. That-that's why you're here! I've got to fire you!
- **fit in**: 融入群体、合群。
  - *Example*: Well nothing yet, they really hate you and I want to fit in.

## 4. 描述事物

- **a two hour train ride away**: 坐两小时火车那么远（形容距离尴尬）。
  - *Example*: Y'know the one from Poughkeepsie, even though she's a two hour train ride away, is really pretty, really smart, and-and a lot of fun.
- **kind of a racist**: 还有点种族主义（Ross 对约会对象的毒舌总结）。
  - *Example*: On the other hand, if she wasn't kidding, she's not fun, she's stupid, and kind of a racist.
- **angry Canadians with no teeth**: 一群没牙的愤怒加拿大人（对冰球运动员的经典吐槽）。
  - *Example*: Well, actually it's a hockey team, so it's angry Canadians with no teeth.
- **covered in marinara sauce**: 浑身沾满意面番茄酱（狼狈至极的画面）。
  - *Example*: Okay! Very funny! Somebody let me out please?! Come on, I'm cold! And covered in marinara sauce!
- **a lean Christmas**: 一个清汤寡水的圣诞节（lean＝瘦、没油水，Joey 被开除后的自嘲）。
  - *Example*: Yep! Looks like it's gonna be a leeeeean Christmas at the Dragon house this year.
- **Magma Ku Laude**: Chandler 把 magna cum laude（优异成绩毕业）说成"Magma Ku Laude"，学霸词汇翻车现场。
  - *Example*: Yeah! You graduated Magma Ku Laude, right? Rachel: No. Chandler: Oh, it doesn't matter.

## 5. 实用短句

- **believe me**: 相信我、我说的没错。
  - *Example*: Well, believe me, it's been a long time since I've been flung.
- **You bet your ass**: 当然、板上钉钉（赌上你的屁股发誓）。
  - *Example*: You bet your ass, I'm gonna fire you! Thank you.
- **my way, or the highway**: 要么听我的，要么走人（独裁式宣言）。
  - *Example*: From now on, it is gonna be my way, or the highway! All right? Does anybody have a problem with that?!!
- **have had it up to here**: 我受够了、忍到顶了（常配手势指头顶）。
  - *Example*: A positive atmosphere! But I-I-I have had it up to here.
- **it never hurts to...**: ……总没坏处（含蓄的建议）。
  - *Example*: Yeah, people get pretty generous around the holidays. And it never hurts to wear tight trousers.
- **make up fake specials**: 编造假特色菜整主厨（服务员们的恶作剧）。
  - *Example*: Because your all gonna make up fake specials and make me cook them like you did the other night?
- **No ma'am**: 不，女士（服软、装乖的恭敬回答）。
  - *Example*: Hey new guy! I said, does anybody have a problem with that?! Joey: No ma'am.
- **There's sooo much you don't know**: 你不知道的多着呢（Phoebe 式神秘）。
  - *Example*: Umm, well I had a similar problem when I lived in Prague. Chandler: Prague? Phoebe: There's sooo much you don't know.
- **Good enough to get fired**: 水平够被开除的了（Joey 式黑色幽默）。
  - *Example*: Monica: Wait, do you know how to waiter? Joey: Good enough to get fired.

## 6. 剧情词汇

- **Poughkeepsie**: 纽约州哈德逊河畔小城，Ross 在火车上结识的女孩住这里，单程两个半小时
- **Montreal / Nova Scotia**: Ross 坐过站一觉睡到蒙特利尔，还遇到一位坐两小时渡轮去新斯科舍的美女
- **Allesandro's**: Monica 担任主厨的餐厅，员工集体排挤她，帽子上被写"Quit, bitch"
- **Patrick**: Chandler 公司游泳选手，家里发明了信用卡磁条，被介绍给 Rachel 后因误会分手
- **Dragon**: Joey 在餐厅给自己取的"酷炫工作绰号"，服务员们都这么叫他
- **Orson Welles**: 传奇导演，Chandler 念成"Orson Wells"，还编出他"雇人只为当众开除立威"的典故
- **Chanukah**: 犹太光明节，Phoebe 的节日歌里给 Monica 连唱好几遍
- **Pumpkin**: Rachel 小时候爸爸叫她的昵称，Phoebe 想找能押韵的名字
- **Rangers / Knicks**: 纽约的冰球队和篮球队，Chandler 靠球票哄 Rachel 和讨好同事
- **401-K**: 美国的退休福利账户，男同事们为争 Rachel 连 401-K 都愿意过户给 Chandler
